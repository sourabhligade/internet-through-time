After-school internet

Separate wing. Year immersions cover search, social, and products. This wing is how people played on the web ~1999–2007: Flash portals, plugin loading bars, Neopets and Club Penguin, high scores in localStorage — not the App Store, not modern HTML5 portals as default.

Sit at XP + IE · type a portal from memory · wait for Flash · play ten minutes · hide the tab when an adult walks by. That ritual was as real as checking email.

Legal / honesty: No ripped commercial Flash binaries. Portal UIs are reconstructions; playable games are museum originals in period genres. Trademarks (Miniclip, Newgrounds, etc.) for historical illustration only.

How to open: use the museum via a local server (python3 -m http.server 8080 → games/index.html) or open games/index.html directly. Do not rely on a bare games/ folder URL in file:// mode (browser shows an empty directory page).
