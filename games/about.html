About this wing

From the late 1990s through 2007, casual gaming on a PC often meant the browser — not Steam’s modern store, not the iPhone App Store (2008), and not console-only culture. Flash Player (Macromedia → Adobe) ran most short games; Java powered long sessions like RuneScape; kids lived in Neopets and Club Penguin.

Plugin story

  • Shockwave / Flash — embed + loading % · “Click to activate”
  • Java applets — older MMOs / embeds
  • Skip Intro — related agency Flash culture (see year Macromedia rooms)
  • Flash end (2020) — we reconstruct portals and ship original JS games; we do not claim a real Flash Player
