About 2006

2006 is when the social web breaks through and platform power begins. Twitter is born. Facebook ships News Feed and opens to everyone. Google buys YouTube late in the year. Digg peaks as the UGC front page. Amazon invents developer cloud. Google builds a web office. Time names “You” Person of the Year. Still pre-smartphone — XP + IE6 mass default.

Scale (Internet Live Stats, June 2006): 85,507,314 websites (+32% from 2005) · ~1.16 billion internet users. Default shell here: Windows XP + IE 6. IE 7 public Oct 18, 2006 (download story, not forced default). Vista RTM Nov — retail mass is Jan 2007 (banned as default here).

Signature products (this immersion)

  • Twitter / Twttr — first post Mar 21 · public Jul 15 · 140 chars · “What are you doing?”
  • Facebook — News Feed Sep 5–6 · privacy controls Sep 8 · open registration Sep 26 (13+ · valid email · 500+ regional networks)
  • YouTube — independent most of the year · Google announce Oct 9 ($1.65B) · close Nov 13
  • Digg — peak UGC year · digg it / bury · power diggers · algorithm drama
  • Google Docs — Writely Mar 9 · Docs & Spreadsheets Oct 10
  • Amazon Web Services — S3 Mar 14 · EC2 Aug
  • MySpace — still mass social (News Corp from 2005)
  • Google Maps — Ajax maps · no Street View (that is May 2007)
  • Reddit — under Digg in cultural mindshare this year
  • Google Reader — Sep 2006 redesign · unread counts (geek RSS)
  • Internet Explorer 7 — Oct 18 download · shell stays XP+IE6 default
