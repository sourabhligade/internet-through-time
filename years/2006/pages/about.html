About 2006

2006 is when the social web breaks through on a fat laptop. Twttr public 15 Jul. News Feed 5 Sep. Facebook open 26 Sep. Google announces YouTube 9 Oct ($1.65B stock) and closes 13 Nov. There is no iPhone.

Scale (dual-cite — do not blend): Internet Live Stats June 85,507,314 websites (+32%) · 1,160,335,280 users · birthmark Twttr. Netcraft hostnames pass 100 million in November (97.9M Oct → ~101M). Shell here: Windows XP + IE 6. IE7 is 18 Oct leftover. Firefox 2 is 24 Oct leftover. Vista retail is 30 Jan 2007.

How a day felt

Boot XP. IE 6. Broadband. Digg or MySpace first. Late year you type 140 characters to 40404. Facebook starts shouting friends’ walls into a feed. YouTube is still independent in June. Developers rent a disk called S3. There is no Home button to swipe.
