What's Cool in 2006

Stuff people actually bookmarked this year — Twttr, News Feed, YouTube after Google, Docs.

  1. Gmail— invite-only · 1 gigabyte · search your mail
  2. Flickr— photostreams + tags (Ludicorp)
  3. Thefacebook— college network only (not open web)
  4. Firefox 1.0— tabs · popup blocking · Nov 9
  5. MySpace— mass HTML social (still larger than Thefacebook)
  6. Google— search habit · IPO year
  7. Bloglines— browser RSS, no install
  8. iTunes Music Store— 99¢ downloads (continuity)
  9. WordPress— self-host blogs
  10. Digg— Dec seed (rise is 2005)
  11. Amazon · Yahoo!— still the big doors

Not yet cool here: YouTube · Twitter · open Facebook · Chrome browser.
