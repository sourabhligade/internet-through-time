About YouTube (2006)

Tagline energy: Broadcast Yourself. The product is easy Flash upload plus a shareable watch page. Founded 2005; 2006 is the year Google buys it — late.

WhenWhat
2005Public beta · Me at the zoo · independent YouTube, LLC
Jan–Sep 2006Independent brand — explode in views · still not Google
Oct 9, 2006Google announces acquisition — $1.65 billion in stock
Nov 13, 2006Deal closes — ownership flips late year only

Two-era rule: Do not treat “Google owns YouTube” as a January 2006 fact. Early/mid year rooms stay independent; late-year copy may mention the deal.

Not yet: modern Material UI · Shorts · algorithmic home · mobile apps as primary. No Street View on Maps; no Chrome; no iPhone.

Connect YouTube to 2006
  • Digg — peak UGC front page links video URLs
  • Twitter · Facebook — social breakthrough year
  • MySpace — mass profiles + embed culture
  • Google Video — Google’s own video product before/alongside the deal
