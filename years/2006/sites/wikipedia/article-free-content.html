Free content

Free content can be used, studied, modified, and redistributed with few restrictions — often under licenses like the GNU Free Documentation License.

Wikipedia's text is framed as free content so readers can reuse knowledge, not only read it once.

Free as in freedom is the slogan energy of the early project — not "free beer" alone.
