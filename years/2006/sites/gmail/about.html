About Gmail (2006)

Announced April 1, 2004. Search-based webmail · conversation view · labels · huge free storage class.

2006 honesty: For much of the public story Gmail is still invite-heavy / prestige beta culture. Open signup for everyone is February 14, 2007 — do not treat “anyone can get Gmail freely” as a year-start 2006 product truth. Invites still circulate; scarcity lore continues.
  • Search, don’t sort — find mail instead of filing forever
  • Conversation view · labels · JS-heavy app-like mail (Ajax culture from 2005 essay)
  • Companion late 2006: Google Docs & Spreadsheets (Oct 10) · Google Reader redesign (Sep)
