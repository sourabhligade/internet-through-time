2006 honesty: News Feed ships Sep 5–6. Privacy controls Sep 8 after backlash. Open registration Sep 26 — anyone 13+ with a valid email can join (500+ regional networks). Not campus-only after that date. Not modern Facebook (no Reactions, Reels, Marketplace default).

Welcome to Facebook

Facebook helps you connect and share with the people in your life — now open beyond college networks.

Launched February 4, 2004 at Harvard as Thefacebook. By late 2006 the product is News Feed + open registration. Mass social is still also MySpace; Facebook is the rising graph.

New this year:

  • News Feed — a stream of friends’ activity
  • Sign up with any email (after Sep 26)
  • Regional networks · privacy controls after the Feed backlash
