Napster Injunction

July 26, 2001: Judge Marilyn Hall Patel orders Napster to stop facilitating transfers of copyrighted music. The company appeals and wins a temporary stay. Shawn Fanning becomes a TIME-cover face of the conflict between free culture and the recording industry. Usage keeps rising even as the legal walls close in.
