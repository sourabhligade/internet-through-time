Markets: Web 2.0 money returns

After the crash years, multi-million (and billion) internet M&A is back on the business page.

  • Yahoo buys Flickr (Mar 20) and later del.icio.us (Dec 9) — social media has buyers.
  • News Corp → MySpace (Jul 18, $580M) — mass social is a media asset.
  • eBay → Skype (Sep 12, ~$2.6B) — communications platform bet.
  • VCs fund YouTube (Sequoia Nov) while TechCrunch covers every seed round.
  • Google IPO afterglow (2004) still frames 2007 optimism — not another 2000 mania narrative yet.
