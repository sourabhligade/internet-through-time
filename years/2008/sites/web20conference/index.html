Web 2.0 Conference

October 2007 · San Francisco · sold-out nexus of the boom (Cybercultural: #1 Web 2.0 moment of the year for some bloggers)

Thesis: Web as platform is no longer a 2004 meme — it’s a business year. Ajax has a name, Maps has mashups, video uploads feel ordinary, and M&A is back. Investors, bloggers, and builders share one ballroom energy.

What “Web 2.0” means in late 2007

  • Ajax apps—Google Maps · Gmail · drag/zoom without full reload
  • Mashups / APIs—HousingMaps then official Maps API (June)
  • UGC platforms—YouTube · Flickr · blogs
  • Crowd front pages—Reddit boosts · Digg digg/bury
  • Tags / folksonomy—del.icio.us · Flickr tags
  • RSS networks—Bloglines · FeedBurner · Technorati
  • Startup press—TechCrunch “Tracking Web 2.0”

Three trails from this room

  1. Ajax / maps trail:Ajax essay→Maps Local Search→HousingMaps
  2. Vote trail:Reddit→Digg→Slashdot (still the old king)
  3. M&A trail:Flickr/Yahoo→MySpace/News Corp→del.icio.us/Yahoo

First Web 2.0 Conference was October 2004 (name sticks). This room is the 2007 sold-out follow-on energy— boom year, not the naming year. Reconstruction only — not O’Reilly’s live site.
