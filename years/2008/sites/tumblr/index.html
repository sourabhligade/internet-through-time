tumblr

Blogs with less fuss and more stuff.

February 2007: Tumblr launches — short “tumblelogs” for quotes, photos, links, and chat posts without the full WordPress install ritual. Internet Live Stats marks Tumblr as a 2007 chart birthmark.

How it felt: reblog culture seed · sparse dashboard · not yet the 2010s fandom machine. Still a desktop habit for most of the year (like Twitter).

Thin museum room — no full dash engine yet. For status streams try Twitter; for long-form Blogger · WordPress.

Ban: modern Tumblr redesign · app-only framing · inventing brand pixels we do not have.
