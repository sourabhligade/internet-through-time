Digg and Reddit: is /. losing the front page?
Posted by CmdrTaco on Tuesday July 12, @10:23AM

2007: Digg’s digg/bury rise and Reddit’s sparse boosts are the year people argue about whether “the front page of the internet” is moving. Peer-ranked news vs editorial green-lighting — commenters already know which side they’re on.

Also this year: Ajax-named Maps, YouTube Flash upload, iTunes podcasts, Firefox still the cool browser. Try the rooms: Digg · Reddit · YouTube.
