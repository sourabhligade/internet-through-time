2007 beat: Slashdot is still the old nerd king — but Digg’s digg/bury rise and Reddit’s sparse boosts are the year people argue about whether “the front page of the internet” is moving.

Digg and Reddit: is /. losing the front page?
from the crowd-votes-dept.
Users digg it / bury stories; Reddit says “boosts.” Commenters swear by moderation scores. Is peer-ranked news better than editorial green-lighting? (Read More…| 428 comments)

Ajax named — Google Maps is the demo app
from the drag-and-zoom-dept.
Jesse James Garrett’s Feb 18 essay; Maps public Feb 8. Suddenly every blog wants an XMLHttpRequest party. HousingMaps pins Craigslist on tiles. (Maps room)

Firefox 1.x still the cool-blogger browser
from the not-ie6-dept.
Mass default is still IE 6 on XP. Extensions, tabbed browsing, and “view source pride” keep nerds on Mozilla’s product.

YouTube: broadcast yourself (Flash upload)
from the bandwidth-hog-dept.
Apr 23 public beta. Ordinary people upload. Dial-up friends wait. Google does not own it yet.

iTunes 4.9: podcasts go mainstream
from the audio-rss-dept.
Jun 28 directory; >1M subscriptions in two days. Diggnation-class shows ride the wave.
