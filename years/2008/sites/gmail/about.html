About Gmail (open since Feb 14, 2007)

How it felt: the exclusive club opens · “finally got one for my parents” · 1GB still felt huge · still wears a beta badge.

Announced April 1, 2004 (many thought it was a joke). Search mail · conversation view · Ajax app-like webmail.

February 14, 2007 — Valentine’s open day: Gmail opens to everyone. Official framing: no more waiting for someone to invite you. Invite scarcity of 2004–2006 ends as the default product story.
Invite nostalgia (legacy): people still talk about trading invites like baseball cards — but in this immersion, Invites is a side story, not the gate. Open signup is the 2007 truth.
  • Search, don’t sort · labels · conversation threads
  • Companion suite energy: Google Docs · Reader · Maps · Street View
  • Beta label drops much later (2009 class) — optional honesty
