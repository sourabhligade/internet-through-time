About del.icio.us

Social bookmarks: save URLs, tag them, discover what others saved. Joshua Schachter founded the service (2003); by 2007 tags / folksonomy are core Web 2.0 vocabulary alongside Flickr photostream tags and blog categories.

December 9, 2007 — Yahoo acquires del.icio.us (TechCrunch / founder blog day). Period user count class ~300,000. Price undisclosed in official posts; period guesses vary. The del.icio.us blog welcomed working with Yahoo Search and “joining our fraternal twin Flickr” (Yahoo had bought Flickr March 20, 2007).

Through most of calendar 2007 the product is independent social bookmarks. The December sale is a year-end M&A beat — same boom as Flickr, MySpace, Skype deals.
