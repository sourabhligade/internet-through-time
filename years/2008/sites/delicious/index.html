del.icio.us

home · about · flickr · 2008 Start

del.icio.us is a social bookmarks manager. Save links, tag them, and see what other people bookmarked the same URL. Folksonomy — tags instead of a hierarchy. Founded by Joshua Schachter (2003); still essential Web 2.0 infrastructure in 2007.

December 9, 2007: Yahoo acquires del.icio.us (~300,000 users in period press). Blog line: joined the Yahoo! family — “excited to be joining our fraternal twin Flickr.” Through most of the year the product is independent social bookmarks.
