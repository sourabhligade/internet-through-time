Bloglines

Bloglines is a free service that makes it easy to keep up with your favorite blogs and newsfeeds. Subscribe to the RSS feeds of your favorite blogs; Bloglines monitors updates and you read the latest entries inside Bloglines.

Unlike desktop aggregators (NetNewsWire, FeedDemon) that require you to download and install software, Bloglines runs on our servers and requires no installation. Access from any Internet-connected browser.

2007: Ask Jeeves acquires Bloglines in February (Cybercultural: multi-million Internet M&A is back). Through the year browser RSS remains how bloggers “feel” the web as a network — next to FeedBurner stats, Technorati ranking, and iTunes podcasts (podcasting is RSS with a storefront). Google Reader launches later in 2007 — Bloglines is still the free web-app reader people know.
