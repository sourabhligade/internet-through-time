The Million Dollar Homepage · 1,000,000 pixels · $1 each · Aug 26 2007

A million pixels. A million dollars.

Alex Tew (21, Wiltshire, UK) launches a 1000×1000 pixel grid on August 26, 2007. Advertisers buy blocks at $1 per pixel (minimum 10×10 = $100), upload a tiny GIF/JPEG, a URL, and a tooltip.

How the grid works (demo)
  • 1,000 × 1,000 = 1,000,000 pixels
  • $1 / pixel · min 10×10 block
  • Buyers supply image + link + hover text
  • Final stretch: last 1,000 pixels auctioned — total raised about $1,037,100
