HousingMaps

Paul Rademacher · ~April 2007 — the first famous Google Maps mashup. Craigslist-class rentals and for-sale listings plotted on the map before Google shipped an official Maps API (API lands June 2007). Google Code Featured Projects highlighted the mashup on April 13, 2007 — reverse-engineered Maps JS, not an official product. Powered by craigslist and Google Maps — this site is in no way affiliated with craigslist or Google (period disclaimer from the live product).
