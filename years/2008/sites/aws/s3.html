Amazon Simple Storage Service (S3)

Launched March 14, 2007 with a press release and a simple blog post — “pay for what you use” object storage for developers.

  • Store and retrieve any amount of data via web services APIs
  • Buckets · objects · REST-style access (period marketing language)
  • Companion story: EC2 in August 2007 — virtual servers by the hour

Exhibit purpose: teach that “the cloud” as a developer product starts here — not as a consumer product, not as a 2020s multi-region console tour.
