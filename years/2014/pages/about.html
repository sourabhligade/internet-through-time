About 2014

2014 is when messaging becomes the mass internet — WhatsApp is the $19 billion install, TLS bleeds, and a summer video loop fills a charity. Still a Win7 / IE 9 laptop. Chrome is the pageview narrative, not this museum’s January chrome.

Scale (dual-cite — do not blend): Internet Live Stats June 968,882,453 websites (+44%) · users 2,925,249,355. 1 billion hostnames first crossed September 2014 (ILS announced · Netcraft October · TBL tweet 16 Sep).
