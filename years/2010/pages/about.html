About 2010

2010 is the year the tablet and the camera-phone filter arrive, while Facebook colonizes the rest of the Web.iPad ships. iPhone 4 brings FaceTime, Retina, and Antenna-gate. Instagram launches iOS-only (filter → share). Open Graph puts Like on partner sites. FarmVille peaks at 83.76 million MAU in March. Desktop Web 2.0 is still how most people live.

Scale (dual-cite — do not blend): Internet Live Stats June 206,956,723 websites (−13% NetCraft class) · 2,045,865,660 users (~2.05B).
Pingdom December 255 million websites · 1.97 billion users (Internet World Stats June). Shell here: Windows 7 + IE 8. XP residual honesty. Chrome / IE9 are product rooms.
