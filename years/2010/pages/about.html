About 2010

2010 is when the tablet and camera-phone app web arrive. The iPad invents a device category. The iPhone 4 brings Retina, FaceTime, and Antennagate. Instagram (Oct 6, iOS only) invents filter-native photo sharing. Facebook hits ~600 million people, ships Open Graph external Like buttons, and becomes a Hollywood story. Foursquare check-in culture peaks. FarmVille peaks near 80M+ MAU. Desktop Web is still how most people live.

Scale (dual-cite): Internet Live Stats June class: 206,956,723 websites (−13% vs 2009 — Netcraft methodology / inactive dip · label the source) · users ~2.05B (Live Stats year). Pingdom end-2010 class: ~255 million websites · ~1.97B users mid-year · Facebook ~600M · YouTube ~2B videos/day. Shell here: Windows 7 + IE 8 mass path; IE 9 is a Sep beta product room; Chrome is a product room, not forced sole shell.
