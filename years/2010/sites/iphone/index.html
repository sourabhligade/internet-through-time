iPhone 4


[wa] Wayback 2010-08-07 · images.apple.com/iphone/apps-for-iphone · period still, not a redrawn stainless-band product shot

WWDC 7 Jun · ships 24 Jun · US $199 16 GB · $299 32 GB · 3GS 8 GB $99 · AT&T 2-year. Retina 960×640 · 326 ppi · FaceTime · 5 MP + LED + HD · stainless band.

FaceTime is Wi-Fi only in 2010. App Store then: 225,000 apps · 5 billion downloads. Not Siri (2011). Not iPhone 4S.

Apple statement · 2 Jul 2010— “Dear iPhone 4 Users” (signed Apple, not Jobs). Gripping the black strip in the lower left corner of the metal band can drop 4 or 5 bars. Apple’s claimed cause in this letter: the formula that calculates bars is totally wrong— often displays 2 more bars than it should. Free software update; 30-day undamaged return. Bumper giveaway is a later mid-July presser, not this letter. Do not put the press slogan “you’re holding it wrong” in this letter’s mouth.
