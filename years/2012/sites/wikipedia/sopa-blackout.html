ENGLISH WIKIPEDIA · JANUARY 18, 2012 · 24 HOURS

Imagine a World
Without Free Knowledge

For 24 hours, English Wikipedia went dark to protest SOPA (Stop Online Piracy Act) and PIPA (PROTECT IP Act) — US bills that free-culture and open-web advocates said could break the web.

Sister protests: Reddit “blackout” energy · Google homepage black bar · other sites dimmed or explained. Museum theater only — no live protest API.
