About 2012

2012 is the year the internet becomes visual and app-first, and Web 2.0’s cultural era ends. Instagram Android 3 Apr then Facebook ~$1B 9 Apr. Facebook IPO 18 May $38. Pinterest public Aug. iPhone 5 / Lightning / Maps. Windows 8 26 Oct — late, not January.

Scale (dual-cite — do not blend): Internet Live Stats June 697,089,489 websites (+101%) · 2,518,453,530 users (3.6 users/site).
Pingdom / Netcraft December 634 million websites · +51 million in 2012 · Pingdom users ~2.4 billion.
Honesty: August 2012 NetCraft dropped 40 million+ wildcard hostnames on 242 IPs — the June +101% is a measurement jump, not “the web doubled culturally.” Shell here: Windows 7 + IE 9 / Chrome rising. Windows 8 is an October product room.

How a day felt

Boot Windows 7 · IE 9 or you’ve already switched to Chrome · Facebook first — friends scream about the IPO and Instagram on Android · pin a wedding board · September: Lightning drawer + Maps put the airport in a lake · October: a cousin installs Windows 8 and cannot find Start · December: Gangnam Style hits 1 billion views · 18 January the encyclopedia went black.
