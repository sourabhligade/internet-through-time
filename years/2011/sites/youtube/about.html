About YouTube (2007)

How it felt: Flash player · buffering · “did you see that video?” · embed everywhere · comments chaos.

Broadcast Yourself under Google ownership all year. The $1.65B deal closed Nov 13, 2006 — so unlike the 2006 immersion, year-start 2007 states Google owns YouTube.

Politics goes UGC: CNN/YouTube presidential debates (Jul Democratic · Sep Republican class) — questions submitted as YouTube videos. Video is no longer only cats and music clips.

Mobile: people try YouTube on the new iPhone Safari (no native App Store YouTube app as default 2007 story). Desktop Flash remains how most people watch.

Not modern Material UI · Shorts · algorithmic home as primary product framing. Not “still independent this year.”
