Qwikster

By May 2011 Netflix was the largest source of Internet streaming traffic in North America — nearly 30% at peak hours (Cybercultural / TechCrunch 17 May 2011). Streaming is the 2011 habit. Qwikster is the funeral.

18 Sep 2011 (TechCrunch / Hastings blog): DVD-by-mail becomes Qwikster. Streaming keeps the Netflix name. Two sites, not integrated. Games by mail. $8 + $8 = $16 if you wanted both. 10 Oct: reversed (~three weeks). SNL mocked the YouTube video. Funeral, not a store you keep using.
