World Wide Web

The World Wide Web is a system of interlinked hypertext documents accessed via the Internet.

Tim Berners-Lee proposed it at CERN; by 2001 it is ordinary office infrastructure and late-night modem habit.

Browsers render HTML; links jump between servers. Search engines (and directories) try to map the mess.
