iPhone 4S

[failed-final] iPhone 4S hero · assets/period/2011 missing

Apple Newsroom 4 Oct 2011 · Philip Schiller on stage · ships 14 Oct · preorder 7 Oct · iOS 5 on 12 Oct. Dual-core A5 (up to 2× CPU / 7× graphics vs iPhone 4) · 8 MP · f/2.4 · 1080p · lock-screen camera · volume-up shutter. First 4S on AT&T + Sprint + Verizon.

Contract: $199 16 GB · $299 32 GB · $399 64 GB. Leftover iPhone 4 $99 · 3GS free on contract. Not iPhone 5.

Siri beta on 4S only — English (US, UK, Australia), French, German. Apple examples: “Will I need an umbrella this weekend?” · “Remind me to call Mom when I get home” · “What’s the traffic like around here?” No speech API in this museum. Not on iPhone 4.

iCloud (same keynote): iTunes in the Cloud · Photo Stream · Documents in the Cloud. Notification Center · iMessage. Steve Jobs dies 5 Oct — last product announced in his lifetime. Honesty, not a shrine.
