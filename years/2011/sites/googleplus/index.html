Google+

Vic Gundotra, Official Google Blog, 28 Jun 2011: “online sharing is awkward. Even broken. And we aim to fix it.” Field trial. Invitation only until public 20 Sep. Not Orkut. Not Buzz. Not Friend Connect.

You do not just sign up. A friend sends an invite. Today’s services “turn friendship into fast food—wrapping everyone in ‘friend’ paper.” Signups hit 10 million / two weeks · 25 million / a month · 40 million Oct · ~90 million EOY — that is not DAU. Google+ did not replace Facebook.

Four pillars (launch post)

+CirclesCollege / parents / boss. Not one “friend” bucket. Sloppy, scary, insensitive — so you share with a circle.
+SparksInterest feed to start a conversation. “Highly contagious content” in over 40 languages. Not the gold machine.
+HangoutsWalk onto the porch. Casual multi-person video, not a scheduled call. Up to 10. No live camera here.
+Instant UploadWith permission, photos leave the phone into a private cloud album. Messenger (name lands 20 Sep) is circle group chat.

+You: “When your invite arrives we hope you’ll join the project. But it’s entirely up to +You.” +1 is not a Facebook Like.
