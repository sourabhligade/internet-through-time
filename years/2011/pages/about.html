About 2011

2011 is the year Google tries to rebuild Facebook as Circles, Spotify finally becomes legal in the United States, and the phone grows a voice. Google+ field trial 28 Jun. Spotify US 14 Jul. iPad 2 has cameras. Timeline 22 Sep. iPhone 4S / Siri 14 Oct.
