About 2011

2011 is the year Google tries to rebuild Facebook as Circles, Spotify finally becomes legal in the United States, and the phone grows a voice. Google+ field trial 28 Jun · public 20 Sep. Spotify US 14 Jul. iPhone 4S / Siri 14 Oct. Timeline 22 Sep.

Scale (dual-cite — do not blend): Internet Live Stats June 346,004,403 websites · users 2,231,957,359. Pingdom December class is about 555 million hostnames — that is December, not June. Shell here: Windows 7 + IE 9. January IE 8 is leftover honesty.

How a day felt

Boot Windows 7. IE 9. An invite to Google+ is a status object. In July Spotify is suddenly legal in the US and you still need an invite. In October you ask the phone for an umbrella. Facebook turns your profile into a memoir. Instagram is still iPhone-only.
