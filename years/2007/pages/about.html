About 2007

2007 is the year the phone becomes a real web browser you carry — while most people still live on the desktop Web 2.0 stack. iPhone announced 9 Jan · ships US 29 Jun · Safari only · no App Store. Gmail opens worldwide 14 Feb. Street View 29 May. Facebook Platform 24 May. Twitter breaks out at SXSW (born 2006). YouTube is Google-owned all year.

Scale (dual-cite — do not blend): Internet Live Stats June 121,892,559 websites (+43%) · 1,373,327,790 users · birthmark Tumblr. Netcraft December 155,230,051 hostnames is a December cell — never mix it into June. Much of 2007 hostname growth is blog-farm (MySpace / Live Spaces / Blogger). Shell here: Windows XP + IE 7. Vista retail is 30 Jan leftover, not this shell. Chrome is 2 Sep 2008.

How a day felt

Boot XP. IE 7. Broadband laptop. MySpace still bigger than Facebook in May. Late June a US store might have a $499/$599 slab that pinches desktop sites. There is no Home-screen grid of apps. Gmail no longer needs an invite. Pegman is five cities. Beacon will scare people in November.
