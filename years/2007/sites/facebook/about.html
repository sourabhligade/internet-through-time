Facebook in 2007

How it felt: still not always #1 vs MySpace · then Platform apps explode · quizzes and SuperPoke · late year Beacon spoils Christmas.

  • Open registration since Sep 26, 2006 — still true all year (not campus-only)
  • News Feed since Sep 2006
  • May 24, 2007 — Platform: third-party applications on the social graph (~24M users class at launch vs MySpace ~67M mass lore)
  • Growth year vs MySpace HTML profiles and Top 8 drama
  • Microsoft invests late year (~$15B valuation class) — money story
Platform ritual: install SuperPoke / quizzes / Causes → notification firehose → learn to remove apps. “You can build a real advertising business on Facebook” (Zuck-to-devs class).
Nov 6, 2007 — Beacon: social-ad system can publish what you do on partner sites (eBay, Blockbuster class) into friends’ News Feeds. Privacy backlash is immediate (MoveOn petition class · Christmas-spoiler lore). Zuckerberg apologizes; opt-out controls follow. Platform opened the graph; Beacon showed how far “share everywhere” could go.
