Firefox 1.x

November 9, 2004 — Mozilla Foundation releases Firefox 1.0. Through 2007 it is the cool-blogger browser: tabs, extensions, popup blocking — while IE 6 on XP remains the mass default shell of this immersion.

Free Download · Features · What's New · NYT ad (Dec 15 2004)

  • Tabbed browsing — multiple sites in one window
  • Popup blocking — you decide when ads interrupt
  • Extensions — greasemonkey-class energy · customize everything
  • Open source · global volunteer community

2007 honesty: Pew / period share class ~8% for Firefox — not “everyone’s browser.” Bloggers, digg users, and Web 2.0 demo videos often show Firefox; corporate desktops still ship IE 6.
Dec 15, 2004: community-funded two-page New York Times ad — see museum strip.
