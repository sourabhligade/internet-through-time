About FriendFeed (2007)

Launched October 2007. Aggregates social and RSS activity so you do not refresh ten sites. Built by people from the Google/Blogger diaspora class. Leads into later “social dashboard” ideas — and pairs with OpenSocial as Google’s multi-network story vs Facebook Platform.
