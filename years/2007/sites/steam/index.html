Steam · 2007

2007 honesty: Steam is no longer a brand-new experiment. Valve’s digital client (public from 2003) is the normal way PC gamers get updates, multiplayer, and store purchases for Half-Life 2–era and third-party titles. Dark chrome · install/update theater · not the modern Discovery Queue / big-sale UI of the 2010s.

What people do in 2007:

  • Install Steam · keep games patched automatically
  • Buy and download from the in-client store (still growing catalog)
  • Friends list / multiplayer matchmaking for Valve titles
  • Still dual-use with retail discs for many players

No real game files or store API. Download button is local progress theater only.

Not yet: mobile Steam · modern big picture only · free-to-play flood as default story.
