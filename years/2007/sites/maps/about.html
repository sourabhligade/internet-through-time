Google Maps in 2007

How it felt: slippy Ajax maps already normal · then pegman drops you on the street · privacy headlines · mashups still cool.

Maps launched Feb 2005. By 2007 the Ajax map and public API are ordinary for geeks. The year-defining leap is Street View (museum date May 29, 2007; some sources say May 25) — ground-level panoramas in five U.S. launch cities: San Francisco · New York · Las Vegas · Miami · Denver.

Street View is a 2007 product fact (banned as default in 2006 immersion). Privacy fights begin the same year. This museum uses theater only — no live tiles.

What people do

  • Local search → directions → zoom (Ajax canvas theater)
  • Drop into Street View and “walk” a block
  • Open mashups like HousingMaps
  • Late year: try Maps on iPhone Safari (EDGE + glass)

Ban: modern Material Maps · live Street View API · claiming global coverage on day one.
