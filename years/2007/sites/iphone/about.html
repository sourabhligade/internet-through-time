iPhone (2007)

How it felt: overnight lines · “three products… one device” · full Safari on glass · EDGE when you’re off Wi‑Fi · still no App Store.

Steve Jobs at Macworld: three revolutionary products — a widescreen iPod with touch controls, a revolutionary mobile phone, and a breakthrough Internet communications device… These are not three separate devices. This is one device. And we are calling it iPhone.
