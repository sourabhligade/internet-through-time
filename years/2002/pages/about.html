2002 — Always-on, not yet “everyone”

Museum year room for the Internet Through Time. Educational reconstruction.

The broadband difference (Pew, June 23 2002)

  • 21% of U.S. internet users have home broadband
  • 24 million adults · equals 12% of all American adults
  • Was only 6% of users in June 2000
  • Among broadband: 71% cable · 27% DSL · 2% satellite/fixed wireless
  • 82% of broadband users online on a typical day vs 58% of dial-up
  • Pew: the net shifts from a “sometimes” tool to an always-on information appliance
  • Excite@Home shut its doors February 2002 — broadband still fragile business

Do not say: “Most adults have broadband.”

Scale

Internet Live Stats (June 2002): 38,760,373 websites (+32% YoY) · 662,663,600 users.

What 2002 is not

  • MySpace (2003) · iTunes Music Store (Apr 2003) · WordPress default (2003)
  • Facebook / Gmail (2004) · Firefox final brand · Netflix streaming
  • Blogger owned by Google · always-on = majority of all adults
