Wired News · About the redesign

October 2002 · all-CSS layout

Wired launches a major redesign that drops table-based layout for validating XHTML structure and CSS presentation — led in the public story by Douglas Bowman (StopDesign). Designers call it a win for web standards on a heavily trafficked, constantly updated news site.

At the same time Flash microsites still define “cool” elsewhere (MTV promos, agency portfolios). 2002 is the split: broadband multimedia and tableless CSS evangelism.
