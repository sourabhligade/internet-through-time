Phoenix 0.1

September 23, 2002 — a standalone browser built on Gecko, spun from Mozilla developers who wanted less suite bloat than Mozilla 1.0 (browser + mail + chat).

This is the path that becomes Firebird, then Firefox — but the Firefox 1.0 brand is still in the future (November 2004). In this year the default story is still IE 6 ~90%.

  • Internet Explorer 6 monopoly sleep
