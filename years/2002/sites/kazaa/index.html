KaZaA

Music at Internet speed — after Napster

By summer 2002, KaZaA (FastTrack) is the mass P2P client people install when Napster is gone. Marketing claims tens of millions of downloads; spyware accusations and KaZaA Lite forks fill forums. Morpheus is kicked from FastTrack and reappears on Gnutella — pure chaos.

  • ~100 million downloads class (Aug 2002 lore)
  • FastTrack peers · search · progressive download UI
  • Still no iTunes Music Store — that is April 2003
  • Legal portable path: iPod gen 2 hardware
