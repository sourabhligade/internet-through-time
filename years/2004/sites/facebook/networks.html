Networks

In 2004 Thefacebook is organized by school networks. You belong to your college graph first — not a global social OS. Brand language: Thefacebook / thefacebook.com era.

NetworkStatus (2004 honesty)
HarvardOpen — day one (Feb 4, 2004)
Columbia · Yale · Stanford classRolling campus expansion spring–fall 2004
Other collegesSchool-by-school gate (not open web)
High schoolsNot yet — 2005 story
Open internet / everyoneNot yet — 2006 open registration
News FeedNot yet — 2006 product
Contrast: MySpace hits ~1 million users around June 2004 and remains the most popular social network for years (Cybercultural). Thefacebook is a campus directory graph — friends’ friends, classes, school search.

From the WA product page: “We have opened up Thefacebook for popular consumption at Harvard University.” Later 2004 = more colleges, still not everyone.
