About Thefacebook

Thefacebook is an online directory that connects people through social networks at colleges. We have opened it for popular consumption at Harvard University first.

February 4, 2004 — launched from a Kirkland House dorm room. Day-one signups in the thousands; by month’s end a large share of Harvard undergrads. Through spring 2004 the product expands to other Boston-area schools and Ivies — still a campus-gated graph, not the open internet.

Founding cast (period histories): Zuckerberg with Saverin, Moskovitz, Hughes, McCollum class. Angel interest and California HQ come later in the year narrative — the product mood here is still college directory.

Not yet: News Feed (2006) · open registration for everyone (2006) · drop-the-“The” / facebook.com rename era (2005). Facemash (late 2003) is a prior footnote only — not this product room.

Mass social in 2004 is still MySpace (~1M users mid-year class). Thefacebook is the campus seed that will grow — not yet everyone’s homepage.
