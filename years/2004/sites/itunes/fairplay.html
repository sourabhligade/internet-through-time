Personal use rights (FairPlay era)

← Store

At launch (Apr 28 2003) Apple framed the Store as own-download with generous personal rights — not a subscription stream.

  • Burn songs onto an unlimited number of CDs for personal use
  • Listen on an unlimited number of iPods
  • Play songs on up to three Macintosh computers (launch rights)
  • Use songs in Mac apps (iPhoto, iMovie, iDVD era)
  • Format: AAC 128 kbps with DRM (FairPlay) — not free unrestricted MP3 dumps

Museum: no real DRM, no real files, no payments. Contrast with KaZaA wild-west P2P still live in 2003.
