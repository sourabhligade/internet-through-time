U.N. and Iraq discuss return of weapons inspectors
Diplomacy desk follows Vienna talks after a four-year inspection gap. Security Council debates dominate wire clusters. (AP)

Israel ends multi-day siege of Arafat compound
International wires report troops leave Ramallah compound after prolonged standoff. Regional coverage runs heavy. (Reuters)

Pew: home broadband hits 21% of U.S. internet users
June report: 24 million adults — still a minority of all adults, but “always-on” habits already differ from dial-up. (Pew Internet)

KaZaA and Morpheus scramble after FastTrack license fight
Post-Napster P2P chaos: Morpheus shifts toward Gnutella; spyware accusations fly both ways; record industry suits continue. (Tech wires)

Mozilla 1.0 ships — suite browser, mail, and chat
June 5: open-source milestone. Developers already talk about a leaner standalone browser (Phoenix later in the year). (mozilla.org)

Google News goes BETA; home page adds News-New! tab
Automated clusters across thousands of sources. Blogs not yet treated as mainstream journalism. (Tech wire)

Apple ships second-generation iPod with touch wheel
