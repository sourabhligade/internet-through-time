AOL-Time Warner

January 2001: America Online agrees to acquire Time Warner for about $165 billion in stock. The New York Times calls it evidence that old and new media are converging. For a few weeks the story feels like a coronation. By spring, Nasdaq's peak and slide reframe the entire year.
