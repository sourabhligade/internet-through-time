KaZaA Media Desktop

Share and search for music, video, and documents with millions of users on the FastTrack network.

By August 2003, KaZaA marketing claimed on the order of ~100 million downloads. The post-Napster wild west also meant spyware accusations, lawsuits, and forks like KaZaA Lite.

Contrast: Legal path arrives April 28 2003 — Apple’s iTunes Music Store at 99¢ per song (AAC + DRM). KaZaA remains the wild alternative. Museum: no real P2P files.
