December 15, 2004 — The New York Times ad

After Firefox 1.0 launched on November 9, 2004, the community funded a two-page New York Times advocacy ad. Thousands of supporters paid small amounts so their names could appear in print — declaring a faster, safer, more open browser was here to stay.
