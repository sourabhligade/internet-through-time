Microsoft Launches Internet Explorer 6
Posted by CmdrTaco on Wednesday September 22, @10:23AM
from the browser-wars dept.

Microsoft held a massive launch event in San Francisco for Internet Explorer 6. IE4 introduces Active Desktop, DHTML, and Channel / push content. Microsofties dropped a giant "e" on Netscape's lawn; Netscape employees toppled it.

The browser is a free download — tens of megabytes on a 28.8 or 56k modem.
