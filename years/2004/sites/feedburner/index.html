FeedBurner

Bloggers burn feeds. Publishers compare subscriber counts. RSS is how the blogosphere feels like a network — the missing stats layer for independent publishers. Cybercultural 2004 year-end: FeedBurner “burst onto the scene” with the one essential service bloggers were missing — a way to track RSS statistics.

Sample dashboard (this browser only)
  • Total subscribers (sample): 12,480
  • Reach (sample): ~38k
  • Item views (sample): 92k

Numbers are local exhibit props — not live analytics. No real FeedBurner account.

Pair with the 2004 reading stack: Bloglines (browser RSS reader) · Blogger · WordPress · del.icio.us · Web 2.0 Conference.

Professionalisation of blogging kicks harder in 2005, but feed stats + browser readers are already the 2004 hinge. Google Reader is not a 2004 product.
