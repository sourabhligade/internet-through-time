* About Me *

Hi, my name is Dave and I live in Ohio. I'm 23 years old and I work at a computer store during the day but at night I watch The X-Files and read about UFOs. I've been building this page since I got my GeoCities homestead in March 1997. I use Netscape Gold to edit my HTML!

I believe we are not alone in this universe. The government knows more than they're telling us. If you feel the same way, you've come to the right place.

* My Favorite X-Files Episodes *

1. "Duane Barry" (S2E05) — One of the most intense episodes ever. The hostage situation is so real and Duane Barry is genuinely terrifying. The ending blew my mind!!!
2. "Clyde Bruckman's Final Repose" (S3E04) — Peter Boyle is amazing in this one. Funny AND creepy. Won an Emmy for a reason.
3. "Jose Chung's From Outer Space" (S3E20) — The funniest episode of the whole series. "That is a bleeping dead alien body!!" LOL
4. "The Erlenmeyer Flask" (S1E24) — The season 1 finale that changed everything. Deep Throat!!!! I can't believe they did that.
5. "Home" (S4E02) — The scariest thing I have ever seen on television. FOX banned it from reruns!!! You HAVE to see this one.
