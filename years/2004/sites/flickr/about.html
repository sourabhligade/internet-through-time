About Flickr

Share pictures in real time. Flickr is a photo-sharing community launched February 10, 2004 by Ludicorp (Stewart Butterfield & Caterina Fake), emerging from the ETech / early Web 2.0 moment.

  • Photostreams — your photos as a living stream, not a buried folder
  • Tags — folksonomy: people invent the index (tags arrive strongly mid-2004)
  • Groups — communities around subjects, cities, cameras
  • Comments & faves — conversation around images
Not yet: Yahoo! does not own Flickr in 2004. Acquisition is March 2005. This room keeps Ludicorp-era pink/blue grammar and independent “social software” buzz.

Cybercultural called Flickr one of the highest-buzz social software apps of the year. Layout class: early clean photo community (Web Design Museum Flickr 2004 gallery).
