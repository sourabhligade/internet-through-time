Wiki

A wiki is a website that lets visitors edit pages in a browser. The word comes from the Hawaiian wiki wiki ("quick").

Ward Cunningham's WikiWikiWeb (1995) inspired later projects. Wikipedia applies the idea to a general encyclopedia anyone can edit.

Pages keep a history of revisions. Vandalism happens; other editors reverse it. The social rules are as important as the software.

See also: Encyclopedia · Nupedia · Help:Editing.
