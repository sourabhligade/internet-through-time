Encyclopedia

An encyclopedia is a reference work giving summaries of knowledge across many subjects.

Print encyclopedias take years and expert boards. Wikipedia tries the same scope with open collaboration and free licenses.

Early 2001 articles are often short stubs. The promise is growth: every blue link can become a page.
