Help:Editing

Basics

  • Click Edit this page on almost any article.
  • Use '''bold''' and ''italics'' (wiki markup theater).
  • Link with [[Page name]] — blue links grow the encyclopedia.
  • Write a short edit summary so others understand the change.

Policies (early spirit)

  • Neutral point of view when you can
  • No original research as dogma — cite when possible
  • Be civil on talk pages (talk is thin in this room)
