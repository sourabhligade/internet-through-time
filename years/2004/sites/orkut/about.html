About Orkut

2004 — Google launches Orkut, a social networking service (named after engineer Orkut Büyükkökten). Invite-heavy growth; early strength in Brazil and India. Profile, friends, communities, scraps.

Not affiliated with Google. No real scraps server. Contrast: MySpace mass HTML · Thefacebook campus · Orkut = Google social.
