Microsoft Internet Explorer 6

Ships August 27, 2001. Bundled story with Windows XP (retail October 25, 2001).

Few flashy new features versus IE 5.5 — an XP-like interface, digital media improvements, P3P privacy support. It wins because nearly every site is tested in IE first.

“IE 6 comes up a winner primarily because of its sheer ubiquitousness.” — contemporary review framing (Thurrott via period press)
