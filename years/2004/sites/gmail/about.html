About Gmail

A Google approach to email. Announced April 1, 2004 as free search-based webmail. Many readers assumed April Fools — 1 GB free sounded impossible next to a few megabytes at Hotmail and Yahoo Mail.

Period product pitch
SearchFind every email with Google search — search, don't sort
Storage1,000 MB (1 GB) free — more than 100× most free webmail
SpeedConversations · stop filing into folders
AdsNo pop-ups · no banners · relevant text ads only
AccessInvite-only beta preview · scarcity / social currency
Three S's: storage, search, speed. No real SMTP here — messages stay in this browser. The word Ajax as a popularized essay name is 2005.

Primary: Google press “Google Gets the Message, Launches Gmail” (Apr 1, 2004).
