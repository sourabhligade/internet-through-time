Welcome to Gmail
A Google approach to email

Gmail is an experiment in a new kind of webmail — never delete mail and always find the message you want.

  • Search, don't sort.
    Use Google search to find the exact message, no matter when it was sent.
  • Don't throw anything away.
    1000 megabytes (1 gigabyte) free storage.
  • Keep it all in context.
    Messages group into conversations with replies.
  • No pop-up ads. No banners.
    Relevant text ads only.

Announced April 1, 2004 — invite-only beta preview. Many thought it was an April Fools joke.
