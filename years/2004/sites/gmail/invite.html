Gmail launched as a preview on April 1, 2004. Free search-based webmail. Storage: up to eight billion bits (~500,000 pages) — 1,000 megabytes class. Search every email. Never file or delete as the primary workflow. Capacity was limited — invitations became social currency: “Hey, I got a Gmail invite — you want one?”
