About the Web 2.0 Conference

October 2004 — San Francisco — the first conference under the “Web 2.0” banner. Target audience: businesspeople and investors, not only developers. Theme: the Web as Platform. Second-bubble energy after the bust.

Period keywords: network effects · architecture of participation · collective intelligence · data lock-in · “eBay envy” · web services · social software. (From Cybercultural memoir of the first conference — O’Reilly / Battelle era framing.)

Earlier the same year (July Oscon), O’Reilly talked network effects and even warned about who would own Gmail data — by October the conference tone embraced commercial network effects. Google’s August IPO sits between those moods.

This room is a period culture reconstruction of conference mood — not a live ticket site or official clone.
