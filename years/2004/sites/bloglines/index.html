Bloglines

Bloglines is a free service that makes it easy to keep up with your favorite blogs and newsfeeds. Subscribe to the RSS feeds of your favorite blogs; Bloglines monitors updates and you read the latest entries inside Bloglines — in the browser.

Unlike desktop aggregators (NetNewsWire, FeedDemon) that require you to download and install software, Bloglines runs on our servers and requires no installation. Access from any Internet-connected browser. (Period pitch class — Cybercultural 2004 called this out as a web-app love letter.)

2004 web-app moment: browser-based readers feel like the future of “social software” alongside Gmail and Flickr. Google Reader is a 2005 story— not here yet.
