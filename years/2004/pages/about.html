About 2004

2004 is the hinge into Web 2.0. Browser-based apps feel native (Gmail), photo sharing + tags explode (Flickr), a campus social graph seeds the future (Thefacebook), Google goes public, the first Web 2.0 Conference sells “the Web as platform,” and Firefox 1.0 (November 9) challenges IE’s peak dominance.

Scale (Internet Live Stats, June 2004):51,611,646 websites · 910,060,180 internet users (~910M).

Four pillars in this year

  1. Gmail— Apr 1 · invite-only · 1 GB
  2. Flickr— Feb 10 · Ludicorp · tags
  3. Thefacebook— Feb 4 · Harvard / college gate
  4. Firefox 1.0+Google IPO
