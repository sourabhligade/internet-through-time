About 2003 — social + 99¢ + self-host + pro network

Post-crash optimism: blogging goes mainstream, Google buys Blogger (Feb), launches AdSense self-serve (Jun 18), and professional blogs find a business model.

  • Friendster public mass (~3M fall) — still the bigger graph
  • MySpace launches August 2003 — HTML-custom profiles; ~100k by October (seed, not king)
  • iTunes Music Store Apr 28 — 99¢ AAC songs, Mac/US first, Windows Oct; 25M songs sold by Dec 15
  • WordPress 0.7 May 27 — b2/cafelog fork, self-hosted PHP/MySQL
  • LinkedIn May 5 — professional network (founded Dec 2002)

Scale (Internet Live Stats, June 2003): 40,912,332 websites · ~778,555,680 users.

Honesty: MySpace launch date sources differ (Aug 1 wiki vs Aug 15 narrative) — we label August 2003. No Facebook product room (Thefacebook is 2004). No Gmail, YouTube, or Firefox 1.0 as defaults (2003 browser side path is Firebird; Phoenix 0.1 was 2002). Friendster founded 2002, mass public 2003. iTunes Store is paid download + DRM, not Spotify-style streaming. RSS: desktop readers plus browser Bloglines.

Footnote: In late 2003 a Harvard student project called Facemash appeared — it is not Facebook / Thefacebook (that product room belongs in 2004). This exhibit does not reconstruct Facemash.
