About this year

2003 is when the social web goes mainstream: MySpace (August), LinkedIn (May), WordPress (May), and Apple’s iTunes Music Store (April 28 — 99¢ a song). Friendster still leads the friend graph. Broadband is always-on. IE 6 on Windows XP is the default browser.

In this immersion: customize a MySpace profile, buy a 99¢ track, publish a WordPress post, add LinkedIn connections. Profile, cart, and tour progress use browser localStorage only — nothing is uploaded.
