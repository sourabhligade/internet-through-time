MySpace · About

An online community that lets you meet your friends' friends

MySpace launches in August 2003 (wiki often Aug 1; some narratives Aug 15) from eUniverse as a fun, HTML-customizable alternative to Friendster.

Through fall 2003 Friendster is still larger (~3 million registered vs MySpace ~100k by October). What MySpace offers: custom profile HTML, looser identity, music-friendly youth culture.
