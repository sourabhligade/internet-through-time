Netscape 6

November 14, 2001 · powered by Gecko · My Sidebar · AIM inside

After years of Mozilla milestones and the skipped “Navigator 5,” AOL ships Netscape 6 — the first consumer Netscape built on the open-source Gecko engine.

  • Integrated browsing, mail, and AOL Instant Messenger
  • My Sidebar channels
  • Standards pitch: HTML 4, CSS1, XML, DOM, JavaScript 1.5

Honest period reputation: ambitious, often buggy, late to a war Internet Explorer already won. Still — for open-web believers, Gecko is the long game.
