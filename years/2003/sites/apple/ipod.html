APPLE · SPECIAL MUSIC EVENT · LATE 2003

iPod

1,000 songs in your pocket.

October 23, 2003: Apple unveils a white-and-chrome hard-drive player with a scroll wheel. Not a phone. Not a store (yet). A jukebox you carry — aimed at Mac users first, with FireWire and a clean iTunes pairing story that began with SoundJam's DNA.

Pocket math (period ad energy):
· 5 GB hard drive
· ~1,000 songs
· Up to 10 hours battery (claimed)
· Mac-first launch window

Napster's court battles remade music culture; the iPod is the legal, shiny counter-spell: your library, portable, paid-for or ripped from CDs you own.
