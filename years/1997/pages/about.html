About This Exhibit — 1997

Late-1997 reconstruction: Internet Explorer 4.0 on Windows 95, browser wars at peak, eBay rebranded from AuctionWeb, Amazon IPO, push channels, DHTML, CNN covers Mars Pathfinder and Princess Diana, Slashdot launches, Apple "Think Different," 56k modems.

What changed from 1996

  • AuctionWeb → eBay (September 1997)
  • Amazon IPO — May 1997, still just books
  • IE 4.0 ships — DHTML, channels, Active Desktop
  • 56k modems replace 28.8k as the standard
  • CNN breaks Mars Pathfinder and Princess Diana coverage online
  • Slashdot, HotBot, Drudge Report emerge
  • Apple launches "Think Different" campaign
  • AltaVista adds Babel Fish machine translation
  • Yahoo! Mail launches — free webmail rivalry (this year: sign-in stamp, not a 1997 inbox)

Timeline

  • Jan: IE 3.02 ships
  • May: Amazon IPO at $18/share
  • Jul: Mars Pathfinder lands; CNN servers overwhelmed
  • Aug: Princess Diana dies — web news explodes
  • Sep: AuctionWeb rebrands to eBay; Slashdot launches
  • Oct: IE 4.0 ships with Active Desktop
  • Dec: ~1.1 million websites; AltaVista Babel Fish
