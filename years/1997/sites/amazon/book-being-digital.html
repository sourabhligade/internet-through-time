Editorial Reviews

Founding director of the MIT Media Lab, Nicholas Negroponte explores the world being increasingly defined by digital technology. From multimedia and virtual reality to digital agents and new forms of expression, Being Digital is a map of our future.

Average Customer Review: ****- (37 reviews)
***** A Must-Read for the Digital Age
By a reader, April 12, 1997
Negroponte's vision of a digital future is remarkably prescient. His discussion of how we'll shift from "atoms to bits" predicted much of what we're seeing today with the explosion of the World Wide Web. Essential reading for anyone in tech.
****- Visionary but sometimes vague
By techreader22, June 3, 1997
Great overview of where digital technology is heading. Some predictions feel very accurate (bandwidth, multimedia), while others are a bit hand-wavy. Still, it's written in an accessible style that non-technical readers will appreciate.
***-- Interesting but dated in places
By bookworm_jane, August 15, 1997
Some of the examples feel a bit outdated already (HDTV standards battles, CD-ROM), but the core thesis about digital convergence remains powerful. Worth reading if you want to understand the big picture.
