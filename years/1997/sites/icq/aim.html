AOL Instant Messenger

May 1997 — America Online launches AIM, a free buddy-list chat client for the open Internet. You do not need a full AOL subscription to use the messenger (you do need to download the program).

  • Buddy lists and idle/away states
  • Instant messages that pop over your work
  • Rivalry with ICQ's growing network
