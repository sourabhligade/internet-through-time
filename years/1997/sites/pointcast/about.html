About PointCast Network

Push is the 1997 buzzword. PointCast delivers news channels to a free Windows client — headlines, tickers, and a screensaver that IT departments love to ban because it chews corporate bandwidth.

How the metaphor works
  • Channel tuner — pick CNN, companies, weather, sports… content “tunes in”
  • Push vs pull — the server pushes; you do not reload a homepage every hour
  • IE4 Channels — Microsoft ships a competing channels story the same year (dirbar Channels)
  • Free + ads — client is free; ads fund the network

This room is educational theater: no real PointCast client runs here. Use the channel guide to feel the push-directory layout.
