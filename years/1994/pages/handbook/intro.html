Heartwarming introduction

Welcome to cyberspace. Really.

The Internet is a network of networks. The World Wide Web is a way of organizing documents on that Internet so you can jump from one to another with a click — hypertext, or when pictures are included, hypermedia.

You don't need to know how TCP/IP works to enjoy a NASA image or a campus directory. But you do need patience: the Net is busy, modems are slow, and many pages are unfinished. That's part of the adventure.
