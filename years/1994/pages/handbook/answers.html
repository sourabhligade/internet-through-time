Handbook

Answers to tough questions

Why is everything so slow?
Modems, busy servers, large images, and a Web that grew faster than backbone capacity.
What does 404 mean?
Not Found — the file moved or the URL is wrong. Try a directory page higher up.
Can I get a virus from the Web?
Downloading and running programs always carries risk. HTML documents themselves are data. Be careful with unknown binaries from FTP.
Who pays for this?
In 1994: universities, government labs, companies experimenting, and hobbyists on shell accounts. The commercial Web is just beginning.
