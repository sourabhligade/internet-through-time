Essentials

To use the Web you need:

  • A computer with a graphical operating system (Windows, Mac, or X)
  • A connection to the Internet (modem + ISP, or campus network)
  • A browser such as Netscape Navigator or NCSA Mosaic

Once connected, you open the browser and either follow a link or type a URL (Uniform Resource Locator) into the Location field.

Example URL:

http://akebono.stanford.edu/yahoo/
