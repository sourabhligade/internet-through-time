What's Cool!

from the Directory menu · Netscape Communications

The What's Cool page highlights some of the most interesting and compelling resources on the Internet. Single-click any blue or purple link to visit.

  • Fish Cam
    A camera pointed at a fish tank — updated images over the Net. Proof that the Web could be playful.
  • Cool Site of the Day
    Glenn Davis picks one cool site every midnight. A daily appointment with the Web.
  • Internet Underground Music Archive (IUMA)
    Independent bands distribute digital audio over the Web. Choose Graphical or dull text. A classic demonstration of multimedia on a 14.4 modem (patience required).
  • HotWired
    Wired magazine online — and home to some of the first banner advertisements on the Web.
  • The White House
    The Executive Mansion arrives on the World Wide Web — publications, tours, and electronic mail to the President.
  • NASA
    Space images, shuttle information, and field centers. Public-domain pictures that made graduate students fill their disks.
  • Yahoo!
    Jerry and David's hierarchical guide at Stanford. The best way to find things when you don't know the URL.
  • The World Wide Web at CERN
    Where the Web began — project overview from the mother site.
  • NCSA Mosaic
    The browser that started it all — and the lab that built it.
  • Personal home pages
    Individuals publishing from university accounts. Under construction. Forever.
