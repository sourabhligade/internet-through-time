A sample of new and noteworthy resources on the Internet. The Web is growing by thousands of servers a year — check back often.

    home.mcom.com — product news, handbook, and demos from Netscape Communications.

    Jerry Yang & David Filo’s hierarchical guide (akebono.stanford.edu/yahoo).

    Independent music over the Net (large downloads!).
