Art museums meet the World Wide Web · 1994		 Start 1994

Before high-resolution zoom viewers, “museum on the Web” meant a few dozen JPEG/GIF paintings, a text label, and a slow download progress bar. French and US institutions experimented with virtual wings — often mirrored on university servers.
