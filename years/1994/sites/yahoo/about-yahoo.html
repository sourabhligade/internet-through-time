About Yahoo!

Yahoo! began in January 1994 as Jerry and David's Guide to the World Wide Web, a list of favorite sites kept by Stanford electrical engineering graduate students Jerry Yang and David Filo.

In April 1994 it was renamed Yahoo! — Yet Another Hierarchical Officious Oracle — because they liked the word (from Gulliver's Travels: rude, unsophisticated, uncouth).

The site ran on Jerry's workstation: http://akebono.stanford.edu/yahoo/ (Akebono was named after a sumo wrestler.) The domain yahoo.com was not registered until January 1995.

Categories were built by hand. Filo argued that no software could beat human filtering. Perl scripts helped generate pages; judgment stayed human.

Yahoo! top

Yahoo! at Stanford is still a human project. Categories are edited by hand. If a link is dead, that is normal — the Web moves faster than any directory.
