Entertainment

Movies, music, TV, humor, and just-for-fun sites. Fan pages often beat official studio pages — when studios have pages at all.

Subcategories

  • Music— Bands, labels, and audio on the Net.
  • Movies— Film information and fan pages.
  • Cool Links— Sites people think are neat.
  • Humor— Jokes, comics, and parody.

Sites

  • Internet Underground Music Archive— Indie music downloads — IUMA
  • Cool Site of the Day— One cool destination each day
  • Netscape What's Cool!— Directory button culture
  • FishCam— Live-ish tank stills from Netscape
  • Movie studio experiments— early promotional pages [elsewhere on the Net]

Yahoo! entries are chosen by people, not a full-text crawler. If a site moved, the link may be stale — that is part of the 1994 Web.
