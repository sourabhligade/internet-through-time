What's New With NCSA Mosaic

This document covers recent changes and additions to the universe of information available to Mosaic (and other WWW browser) users. Entries below are a Selected announcements from recent What's New postings.

Announcements (sample, 1994)

  • Yahoo! — hierarchical guide to the Web (Stanford)
  • The White House interactive citizens' handbook
  • NASA public information servers expand
  • Internet Underground Music Archive — indie music online
  • HotWired — Wired magazine online
  • Cool Site of the Day
  • CERN World Wide Web project pages
  • Lycos catalog at Carnegie Mellon
  • University home pages, departmental servers, and personal ~user pages continue to appear daily
  • Online museum experiments, campus maps, and technical reports go hypertext
  • More federal agencies join the Web — see White House gateway lists
  • New servers announced on Usenet comp.infosystems.www.* almost every day

At the start of 1994 there were only a few hundred Web sites in the world. By December, researchers counted on the order of ten thousand.
