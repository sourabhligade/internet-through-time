About NCSA

NCSA Home

The National Center for Supercomputing Applications is located at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. NCSA is one of the National Science Foundation's supercomputer centers, supporting researchers across the United States.

Beyond supercomputers, NCSA became famous in the early 1990s for software that made networked information usable by non-specialists — especially Mosaic.

NCSA builds software for scientists and, famously, Mosaic — the browser that made the Web visible to people who were not physicists. Documentation is earnest, technical, and free.
