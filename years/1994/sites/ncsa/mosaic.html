NCSA Mosaic

NCSA Home

NCSA Mosaic is a distributed hypermedia browser designed for networked information. It displays formatted text and inline images in one window, and understands multiple Internet protocols including HTTP, FTP, Gopher, and news.


Platforms

  • Mosaic for X Window System (Unix)
  • Mosaic for Microsoft Windows
  • Mosaic for Apple Macintosh

History

Developed at NCSA by a team including Marc Andreessen and Eric Bina. Version 1.0 for X appeared in 1993; Windows and Mac ports followed. By 1994 Mosaic was called the "killer app" of network computing.

In April 1994, Andreessen and others left to form Mosaic Communications Corporation (later Netscape). NCSA continues Mosaic development separately.
