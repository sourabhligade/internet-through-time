Coin

money · markets · the Net as storefront

Can you sell anything on a network designed for physics papers? October 1994: banner ads appear. AT&T asks if you have ever clicked your mouse “right HERE.” Some readers are offended. Some click. The CPM is invented in real time.

Commerce needs trust, catalogs, and credit cards that work over slow modems. None of that is finished. Secure servers are a promise more than a habit. The experiment is the product.

By late 1994, “online commerce” still meant catalogs, 800-numbers, and a few brave credit-card forms. HotWired’s banners proved attention could be sold; shipping products was tomorrow’s problem.

Coin files short; the Net is still deciding how money works here.
