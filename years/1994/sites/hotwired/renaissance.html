Renaissance

arts · museums · sound on a phone line

Museums put collection photos online. Musicians bypass labels via IUMA. A student in a dorm can publish to the planet before breakfast.

The tools are crude: gray backgrounds, 13 KB GIFs, MIDI files that seize the sound card. The feeling is not crude. For a moment, publishing and audience share the same wire.

Places to look

  • NASA images — public-domain space stills (patience required)
  • IUMA — indie bands, Graphical or dull text
  • CERN / W3 — where hypertext met the Internet
  • Exploratorium — hands-on science online
  • WebLouvre — art on the early Web
