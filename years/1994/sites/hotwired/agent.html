Intelligent Agent

culture · criticism · the personal Web

Culture on the Web still looks like term papers with blue links. That is the charm. Personal home pages are diaries without a lock. Fan sites outrank corporations. Nobody has figured out “voice,” so everyone shouts in <blink> and under-construction GIFs.

This section watches art, zines, and the people who treat HTML as a material — not a memo format. We link to messy dorm pages and careful museum experiments with the same curiosity.
