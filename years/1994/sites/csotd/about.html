About — Cool Site of the Day Home

A person, not a ranker, picked one URL before midnight.

Glenn Davis started Cool Site of the Day in August 1994. Each night a new address flooded a 14.4 modem. Tomorrow the flood moves. There is no search box that decides “cool.”

This museum room is three pages — the 1994 ritual, not a stamp:

  1. Today — one pick. Click it (wander). Then sign the guestbook.
  2. Archive — last week’s in-year rooms. Clicks here do not stamp.
  3. About — this page. The editor, not an algorithm.
What this is not
  • Not Yahoo!’s whole directory (that’s the other 1994 machine)
  • Not a BBS — last call on Night Owl is the board ritual; textfiles.com is a 1998 website
  • Not a 2026 “best of the web” list
  • Not a visit counter that writes when you only click

Empty guestbook name never writes itt94-csotd. Wander first.
