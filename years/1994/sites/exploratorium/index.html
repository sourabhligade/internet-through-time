Exploratorium
Museum of science, art, and human perception · San Francisco · on the Web, 1994		Home

The Exploratorium was among the first science museums to treat the public Web as an exhibit hall — not just a brochure. Early pages mixed plain HTML, GIF diagrams, and “click here to learn more” experiments that ran slowly over 14.4 kbps links.
