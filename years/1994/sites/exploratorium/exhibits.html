Online Exhibits (sample)

Early “online exhibits” were often short HTML essays with a diagram GIF and a list of links. Full Java applets and Shockwave would arrive later; in 1994 the medium is hypertext + patience.

Featured experiments
SeeingColor, afterimages, optical illusions — describe what you see, then read the physics.
HearingSound waves, beats, and “can you hear the missing fundamental?” (text + small AU later).
Touch & balanceProprioception notes — hard to demo on a modem, easy to write about.
MemoryShort quizzes in forms that post to… nowhere yet. Print the answers.
