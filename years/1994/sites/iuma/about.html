About IUMA

The Internet Underground Music Archive was founded in 1993 by Rob Lord, Jeff Patterson, and Jon Luini at the University of California, Santa Cruz. It began on FTP, Gopher, and Usenet, then moved to the World Wide Web around November 1993–early 1994.

Goal: give independent musicians distribution without a major label. CNN featured IUMA in March 1994. The Computer Paper called it "the closest thing to a pirate radio station" — except the music was downloaded, not broadcast.

Pronounced I-yoo-MAH.
