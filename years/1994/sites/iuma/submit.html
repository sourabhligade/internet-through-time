How to Submit Your Music

In 1994, the only way onto IUMA was to mail a tape of original material. Uploading random MP2s was not allowed — the team encoded files by hand.

  1. Record original music you have rights to distribute
  2. Mail a cassette (or DAT) to IUMA at UC Santa Cruz (historical address)
  3. Include contact info and a short bio
  4. Wait — encoding and page building took time

MPEG encoding software existed for under $100, but IUMA handled conversion for artists.
