Legal Issues

Artists must submit original material they have the right to distribute. IUMA was not a pirate site — it was an alternative distribution channel for independents.

Listeners: files are for personal use as offered by the artists. Don't bootleg commercial CDs onto the archive.

IUMA hosts material submitted by artists. Copyright stays with the creators. Do not redistribute commercial major-label tracks from this archive — this is the independent underground on purpose.
