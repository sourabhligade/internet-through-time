Help / FAQ

How do I hear the music?
Download an MP2 file, then open it with a helper application that understands MPEG audio. Browsers in 1994 did not stream inline like modern sites.
Why is it so slow?
14.4 kbps modems move about 1–2 KB per second in real conditions. A 2 MB file is a long wait.
Graphical vs dull text?
Graphical mode includes logos and album art. Text mode is faster on slow links.
Can I upload my MP3?
MP3 was not the early IUMA format — MP2 was common. Uploads were not open; mail a tape.
