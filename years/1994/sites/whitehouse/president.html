Welcome from the President

White House Home

I am pleased that the White House is joining the World Wide Web. Technology is changing how Americans work, learn, and communicate. The Information Superhighway should serve every citizen — in schools, libraries, hospitals, and homes.

I hope you will use this service to learn more about your government, and to share your ideas with us.

— President Clinton

(Paraphrased welcome in the spirit of the 1994 launch; not a verbatim archived letter.)
