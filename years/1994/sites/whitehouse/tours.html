Tours of the White House

White House Home

In person

Public tours of the White House are available for U.S. citizens through members of Congress, and for others through their embassies. Tour schedules change; contact your representative for current information.

Online

This Web site offers a simple introduction to the White House rooms and grounds. In 1994 this was one of the first attempts at a "virtual tour" of a public building.

  • The East Room
  • The State Dining Room
  • The Blue Room, Red Room, and Green Room
  • The Oval Office (not on public tour)
  • The Rose Garden and South Lawn
