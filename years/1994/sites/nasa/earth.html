Mission to Planet Earth

NASA Home

NASA studies Earth's atmosphere, oceans, ice, and land using satellites and airborne science. “Mission to Planet Earth” is the 1990s name for this effort (later reorganized as Earth Science programs).

  • Earth Observing System (EOS) planning and early missions
  • Atmospheric science and ozone studies
  • Oceanography and climate research
  • Data sets shared with universities — often FTP, sometimes Web forms

Remote-sensing images can be megabytes. Prefer the text summaries first; fetch imagery when you can leave the phone line free for a while.
