Space Shuttle Status

NASA Home

The Space Shuttle fleet provides reusable access to low Earth orbit for crews and cargo. Status pages in 1994 were mostly text — mission timelines, orbiter assignments, and landing weather notes — not live video. Large photo sets live under Images; load them only if you have time on a 14.4 modem.

Orbiters

  • Columbia (OV-102) — first orbiter to fly
  • Discovery (OV-103)
  • Atlantis (OV-104)
  • Endeavour (OV-105) — built as a replacement after Challenger

Where information comes from

Launch and landing information is issued by Kennedy Space Center and Johnson Space Center. University mirrors and NASA centers often copy the same bulletins. If a link is busy, try again later — public interest spikes around launches.
