About Flickr · Home · Explore · Groups · Tags

Flickr is photo sharing: free accounts, photostreams, tags, groups, comments, favorites. Period energy: share pictures · browse everyone’s photos · folksonomy tags.

Launched February 10, 2004 from Ludicorp. Yahoo acquired Flickr/Ludicorp on March 20, 2005 (~$22–25M class). Through 2007, Yahoo ownership is an established fact — the community flavor and API culture continue; this is not a sudden merge into Yahoo Photos.

2007 continuity: Still the photo network people mean when they say “tags.” Companion bookmarks: del.icio.us (Yahoo bought Dec 9, 2005 — “fraternal twin” energy into 2007). Portal: Yahoo!.
