About Vine (2013)

January 24, 2013 — Vine launches on iOS after Twitter’s acquisition (~$30M class). Format: up to 6 seconds, hold-to-record, seamless loop.

Android follows June 2, 2013. Windows Phone class later in the year. Comedy, music, and meme loops define the product.

Not TikTok · not Instagram Reels · not multi-minute video. Educational reconstruction only.
