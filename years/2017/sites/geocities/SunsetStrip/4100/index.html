♥ Concert Reviews ♥

Radiohead @ Polaris Amphitheatre, Columbus OH
August 15, 1997

OH MY GOD. This was the best show I have EVER been to!!!!! They opened with "Lucky" and the sound was incredible. When they played "Paranoid Android" the whole crowd went insane. Thom Yorke's voice is even more amazing live. They played almost all of OK Computer plus older stuff like "Creep" and "Fake Plastic Trees." I was crying during "Exit Music (For a Film)" it was so beautiful. If you EVER get a chance to see Radiohead live DO IT.

Rating: 10/10 !!!!!!!

Foo Fighters @ Newport Music Hall, Columbus OH
June 22, 1997

Such a fun show!! Dave Grohl has so much energy it's insane. They played a bunch of stuff from The Colour and the Shape which just came out. "Everlong" live is incredible. The venue was small and sweaty and perfect. Got a setlist from the stage!!

Rating: 8/10
