In 2000 a redesign adds a blogroll — famously titled something like “Not Recommended At All” — the ironic Gen-X way to point newcomers at other weblogs. Click a link, hit Back, repeat: discovery without a portal editor.

The site sits in the same story as CamWorld, MetaFilter, and Blogger: personal voice, outbound links, daily rhythm, early RSS experiments. No venture deck — just a person posting links and short essays from a browser.

What you do here: read sample posts, wander the archive, hop the blogroll to CamWorld or MetaFilter. This is the open web's 2000 rhythm: small pages, big curiosity, zero feed algorithms.
