About Google

Google's mission is to organize the world's information and make it universally accessible and useful. Founded September 1998 by Larry Page and Sergey Brin. Sparse home page — no portal litter.

2000 moment: still word-of-mouth growth vs Yahoo/MSN Media Metrix leaders. PageRank, dynamic snippets, cached pages, and I'm Feeling Lucky define the product. Ads stay off the classic home page in this reconstruction.

Technology: Stanford roots · June 1999 $25M equity (Sequoia / Kleiner) · Netscape Search experiments with Google tech alongside Open Directory.

Logo: WA mid-2000 harvest where available; see CAPTURE-LOG / ASSETS.
