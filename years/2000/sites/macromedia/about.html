Flash 5 · ActionScript

Macromedia positions Flash as the interactive web runtime. Designers ship intro movies; users invent Skip Intro. Player install rate becomes a plug-in arms race with Real and Windows Media.

2000 product story: timeline animation, symbols, and ActionScript that lets developers build menus, games, and branded experiences that HTML tables cannot match. Bandwidth still hurts — a 200 KB intro is a long wait on dial-up.
