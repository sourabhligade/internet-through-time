Homestar Runner· About

Flash comedy series (early web fame)

By mid-2000 Homestar Runner is the kind of hand-drawn Flash site friends forward in email: Strong Bad, menu screens, and toons that only work if you installed the Macromedia plugin.

It sits next to Skip Intro agency culture — except this one people want to watch. No full episode archive is stored here; the point is the ritual: splash, menu, wait for the plugin.

  • Flash 4/5 timeline animation as mass culture, not just ads
  • Character world that grows into later Strong Bad Email fame
  • Same plugin arms race as Macromedia Flash
