MetaFilter · about

A community weblog

Founded 1999 by Matt Haughey. By 2000 MetaFilter is a known hangout: members post interesting links, everyone comments, the front page is a shared reading list for people who live on the open web.

How it works: someone finds a page worth talking about, posts a short blurb and URL, and the thread becomes the product. Invite/membership culture evolves; the tone is smart, skeptical, and link-first — not a corporate portal channel.

Peers in this year: CamWorld, kottke.org, Slashdot, Blogger personal sites.
