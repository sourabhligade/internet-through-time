Your Rights Online: RIAA sues Napster as campus P2P explodes
Posted by CmdrTaco · from the free-as-in-beer dept. · 2000

June 2000 — The recording industry wants a preliminary injunction as Napster hits mass culture. Universities choke on bandwidth. Is a search index the same as hosting the files?
