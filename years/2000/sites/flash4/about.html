Splash pages & the plugin gate

Macromedia ships Flash 4 in 1999; by 2000 design schools teach timeline animation and clients demand intros. Accessibility and search engines lose. The plugin becomes a cultural gate: if you do not have it, the site is a black box.

Skip Intro is the folk UI of the year — users invent the button agencies forgot. ActionScript in Flash 5 (2000) pushes richer interactivity; same arms race as RealPlayer and Windows Media.

Related rooms: Macromedia Flash 5, Homestar Runner (people actually want the plugin for comedy).

No executable SWF is loaded here — buttons only simulate the splash ritual.
