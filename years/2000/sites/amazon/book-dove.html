Book Description

At sixteen, Robin Lee Graham set sail from Southern California in a 24-foot sloop named Dove. Five years and 33,000 miles later, he completed a solo circumnavigation of the globe — the youngest person ever to do so. This is the true story of that remarkable voyage: the storms, the loneliness, the beauty of remote islands, and the romance that sustained him.

Originally serialized in National Geographic, this modern adventure classic continues to inspire dreamers and sailors worldwide.
