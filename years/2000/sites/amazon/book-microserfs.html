Book Description

A brilliant novel about life at Microsoft — and beyond. Daniel, a code-testing drone at Microsoft, chronicles the lives of his fellow "microserfs" as they navigate the Redmond campus, subsist on Costco junk food, and dream of escape. When they finally break free to start their own Silicon Valley startup, everything changes. Funny, poignant, and eerily accurate about tech culture.

Coupland absolutely nails the culture of working at a big tech company. The diary format works perfectly. I laughed out loud at the Jell-O references and the flat foods ranking. If you work in tech, you'll recognize every character.
