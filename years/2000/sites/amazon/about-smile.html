Why the smile is 2000

Turner Duckworth's A→Z smile arrow becomes the consumer brand this year. For Amazon, 2000 is the first year the curved smile wordmark is the correct logo — earlier years kept the flat pre-smile mark.

Tab insanity: multi-store navigation (books, music, DVD, toys, electronics, auctions, zShops) stretches the red header into category chaos. Shoppers bounce across tabs while 1-Click and gift services stay in the cart path.

Marketplace / zShops: third-party sellers grow beside the core catalog. Session cart and checkout stay in this browser only — no real charges.

Logo assets may be period crop / continuity pack — never use a pre-smile wordmark as the 2000 default brand.
