X.com + Confinity → PayPal

March 2000 merger of Elon Musk’s X.com online bank ambitions with Confinity’s PayPal money product. Brand consolidation continues through the crash year while auction volume keeps P2P payments relevant.

  • Not a modern “app bank” — email-era web forms
  • Trust/fraud language appears early (chargebacks, spoofed email)
  • No real bank transfers — balances stay in this browser.
