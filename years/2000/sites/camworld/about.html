CamWorld · About

Cameron Barrett's daily filter

CamWorld is one of the signature early weblogs: short link posts, personal commentary, and a sidebar full of “Sites I Visit Often.” In 2000 that blogroll is how you find the rest of the small web without a portal editor deciding for you.

Tone is dry, tech-adjacent, and link-heavy — the same culture that later fills Blogdex and Technorati. Readers hit refresh for new outbound links more than for long essays.

Blogroll era: lists of other people’s sites are the social graph. See Sites I Visit Often for the in-year hop list (kottke, MetaFilter, Slashdot, Blogger).
