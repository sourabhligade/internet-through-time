Pets.com timeline

The sock puppet is 2000 crash culture in one mascot: Super Bowl fame, IPO, then November shutdown. Unit economics never closed — free shipping and TV ads outran pet-food margins.

Feb 2000IPO — public markets price the dream
Mar 10 2000NASDAQ peak; long deflation begins
