About 2000 — peak, crash, culture still loud

Y2K fades in January. AOL–Time Warner (~$165B, Jan 10–11) is sold as Internet triumph. The NASDAQ peaks March 10 (~5,000) then deflates through year-end. Failure culture and Pets.com become memes — while Flash, blogs, and Napster keep the web feeling alive.

  • Amazon smile (Turner Duckworth) — first year the A→Z smile is correct · tab insanity
  • Napster monster year (~10M by May) · RIAA · Patel injunction Jul 26 · not streaming
  • Gnutella Mar 14 — decentralized P2P escapes Nullsoft
  • Flash 5 (Aug) + ActionScript · splash pages · Homestar Runner
  • IE 5.5 mid-year · Windows ME Sep 14 · Netscape 6 Nov 14 (secondary)
  • PayPal / X.com merge March 2000

Scale (Internet Live Stats, June 2000): 17,087,182 websites · ~413,425,190 users.

Honesty: No Wikipedia (2001). No Windows XP / IE 6 default. No iPod or iTunes Music Store as shipped products (SoundJam→Apple path only). Napster is P2P culture war, not Spotify. Dial-up still home majority.
