What this year is

Mid–late 1999: Windows 98 SE, Internet Explorer 5.0, Napster, Blogger, funded Google, Yahoo!–owned GeoCities, multicolor eBay, multi-category Amazon, and Y2K anxiety. Portals still win everyday attention; Google is rising among people in the know.

How to explore (2 minutes)

  1. Skip dial-up or watch it once.
  2. Use the yellow tour or Links bar: Napster → Google → Blogger → Yahoo → Amazon → eBay.
  3. Type shortcuts in Address + Go: napster, google, blogger, yahoo, y2k…
  4. Favorites menu stores bookmarks for this year only (localStorage).
  5. Exit (top strip) returns to the hub year menu.
