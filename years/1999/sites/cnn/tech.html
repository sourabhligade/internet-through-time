Microsoft monopoly findings of fact

November 5, 1999 — Judge Thomas Penfield Jackson finds that Microsoft possesses monopoly power in Intel-compatible PC operating systems and took actions to crush threats to that monopoly, including Netscape's browser and Sun's Java.

The case, filed in May 1998 by the Justice Department and states, centers on Internet Explorer bundling with Windows. Bill Gates and Microsoft deny wrongdoing. Conclusions of law and remedies come later.
