About Napster

Napster is a real-time search engine for MP3 music files on the computers of other users. We index what people share — we do not host the music files on our servers.

Created by Shawn Fanning. Mid-1999 launch. By December the RIAA files suit for contributory and vicarious copyright infringement — a flashpoint between the music industry and Internet culture.

MP3 Copyright Policy (summary)
Napster supports the rights of artists and copyright holders and seeks to comply with applicable laws. Report infringement via our policy contacts (period framing).
