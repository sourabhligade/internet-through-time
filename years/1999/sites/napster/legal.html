Legal pressure · late 1999

Napster’s client lets you search MP3s on other people’s computers and transfer files peer-to-peer. Founders describe it as a real-time search engine — an index, not a store of songs on Napster’s own disks.

  • Dec 6, 1999 — RIAA sues Napster for contributory and vicarious copyright infringement.
  • College networks strain under “music at Internet speed.”
  • Centralized index (later systems like Gnutella push further decentralization).

Search and download controls in this exhibit show peer lists and progress UI only — they do not transfer real audio files.
