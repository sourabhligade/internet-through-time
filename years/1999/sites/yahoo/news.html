Top Stories

Mars Pathfinder Sends First Color Photos
NASA's Mars Pathfinder lander and Sojourner rover successfully transmit stunning color images from the Martian surface, captivating millions following along on the World Wide Web. (Reuters)

Microsoft Launches Internet Explorer 5.0
Microsoft officially releases Internet Explorer 5.0 with Active Desktop integration, escalating the browser wars with Netscape Navigator. The launch event in San Francisco features a 30-foot IE logo. (AP)

Apple Unveils "Think Different" Campaign
Apple Computer launches its iconic "Think Different" advertising campaign featuring images of revolutionary thinkers, signaling a new direction under returned CEO Steve Jobs. (Reuters)

Amazon.com Goes Public in Major IPO
Online bookseller Amazon.com completes its initial public offering at $18 per share, giving the Seattle company a market valuation of $438 million despite having never turned a profit. (AP)

eBay: From AuctionWeb to Online Trading Giant
Pierre Omidyar's online auction site, formerly known as AuctionWeb, officially rebrands as eBay and reports hosting over 800,000 auctions daily. (AFP)

Deep Blue Defeats Kasparov in Historic Chess Match
IBM's Deep Blue supercomputer defeats world chess champion Garry Kasparov in a six-game match, marking the first time a computer beats a reigning champion under standard conditions. (AP)
