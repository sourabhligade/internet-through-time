Book Description

In December 1999, a multinational team journeys out to the stars, to the most awesome encounter in human history. The film adaptation starring Jodie Foster opens July 1997. Carl Sagan's spellbinding novel is at once a work of dazzling scientific speculation and an enthralling story of first contact with an extraterrestrial civilization.

Now a major motion picture! The film directed by Robert Zemeckis brings Sagan's vision to the big screen this summer.
Customer Reviews

***** Sagan at his finest
By stargazer_tom, February 14, 1997
A beautiful blend of hard science and philosophical wonder. Sagan makes you feel the vastness of the universe and the smallness of our place in it. Ellie Arroway is one of the best characters in science fiction. Read the book before seeing the movie!
***** Better than the movie
By scifi_fan99, August 2, 1997
Just saw the Jodie Foster film and had to re-read the book. The novel has so much more depth — the Machine carries five passengers in the book, not one! Sagan's scientific detail is amazing and the ending is even more profound on paper.
****- Thought-provoking
By curious_mind, September 10, 1997
The science is fascinating and the questions about faith vs. evidence are handled with great nuance. Pacing drags a bit in the middle with the political machinations, but the payoff is worth it.
