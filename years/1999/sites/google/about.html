Company · 1999

June 7, 1999: Google raises about $25 million from Sequoia Capital and Kleiner Perkins. The homepage stays sparse — a search box, I’m Feeling Lucky, and little of the portal chrome Yahoo and Excite pile on.

Why people try it: relevance from PageRank; fewer “paid placement” traps than some rivals. Index size studies still put Google mid-pack — quality over “we crawled more pages.”

What this is not: not your default homepage for most users yet; not Gmail; not Maps; not the colorful doodle empire.
