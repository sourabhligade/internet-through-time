My Netscape · channels · personalization · 1999 Home

My Netscape is the personalizable Netcenter homepage: drag modules, pick news channels, stock tickers, and weather. In March 1999 Netscape publishes RSS 0.90 so sites can syndicate headlines into My Netscape — the quiet start of the feed web.
