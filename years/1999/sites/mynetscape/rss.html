RSS 0.90 (1999)

RDF Site Summary — a simple XML format for channel metadata and item headlines. Publishers put a netscape.rdf (or similar) file on their server; My Netscape pulls titles into a module.

<rss version="0.90">
 <channel>
 <title>Example News</title>
 <link>http://example.com/</link>
 <item><title>Headline one</title></item>
 </channel>
</rss>

Later years add RSS 0.91/2.0 and Atom; aggregators leave the portal. In 1999 the innovation is “headlines come to you.”
