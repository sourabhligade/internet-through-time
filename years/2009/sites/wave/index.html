Google Wave

Announced May 28, 2009 at Google I/O — the same day Microsoft unveils Bing. Famous ~80-minute demo: real-time collab that looked like email + docs + IM, character-by-character typing. Geeks begged for invites. Almost nobody used it as daily email in 2009.

Honesty: Invite / demo lore only this year. General public mass is May 2010, then the product fades. Do not treat Wave as 2009 everyday inbox. Gmail stays the open mail product.
