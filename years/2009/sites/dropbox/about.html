Why Dropbox is on the 2009 chart

Internet Live Stats lists Dropbox among 2009 website-count birthmarks next to the 172,338,726 sites figure. This museum room is that story — not a claim that Dropbox already owned consumer file sync the way it would later.

Product feel 2009: beta invite energy · a desktop folder that “just works” · free tier small · still competing with email attachments and USB keys for normal people.
