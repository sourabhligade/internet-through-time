Facebook in 2009 — social goes mainstream

The year of the Like button, beating MySpace in US uniques class, buying FriendFeed for real-time feed energy, and finally killing Beacon. Platform games (especially FarmVille) turn the feed into freemium spam culture. End-year class ~350 million people · ~50% daily login (Pingdom).

Beacon honesty: Beacon launched in 2007 (Beacon ads / share-with-friends blowups). 2009 is the death (Sep 21 announcement), not the birth. Do not frame Beacon as “new in 2009.” Connect (from 2008) stays the cool partner-login story; Beacon is the cautionary tale.
