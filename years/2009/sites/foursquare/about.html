Foursquare (2009)

Dennis Crowley & Naveen Selvadurai. Public launch Mar 11, 2009 at SXSW Austin. Location check-ins · points · badges · mayorships · leaderboard. Hailed as “the next Twitter” in period press. iPhone-first culture.

Scale honesty: Thousands of users post-SXSW — not millions day one. 1M users class is a 2010 story. Fake venues here · no live GPS · no real mayor fights.

Seed of the 2010s location / check-in stack. Pair with Kickstarter and WhatsApp as “seeds of the 2010s.”
