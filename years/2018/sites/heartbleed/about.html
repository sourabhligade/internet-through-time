What was Heartbleed?

  • TLS/DTLS heartbeat extension implementation bug
  • OpenSSL 1.0.1 through 1.0.1f (+ 1.0.2-beta class)
  • Public disclosure ~ 2014-04-07 with patch 1.0.1g
  • Mass culture: “change your passwords” on the open web
