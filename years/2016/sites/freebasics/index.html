February 8, 2016 — TRAI bans differential pricing for data. Facebook withdraws Free Basics in India February 11.

Same bits, same price

Zero-rating in 2016 voice: some apps ride free on a carrier plan; the rest of the web costs data. That is not “the internet for free.” It is a walled garden with a free sticker.

An 11-month fight — SaveTheInternet — ended with a rule: the same bits, the same price. Free Basics is not “India banned Facebook.” Facebook the social network stayed.

Honesty: Free Basics still ran in other countries. This room is the India / open-web story, not a global product funeral.
