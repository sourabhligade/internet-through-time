Is there an iTunes Music Store?
Yes — open since April 2003. Buy songs and sync to iPod via iTunes on your computer.

Does the iPhone replace the iPod in 2007?
iPhone (ships Jun 29) also plays music, but App Store live Jul 2009 yet. Most people still buy/use iPods as dedicated players.

Does it replace KaZaA?
It is the legal paid path. P2P still exists; Store + CD rips are the Apple story.
