About Blogdex

MIT Media Lab · Cameron Marlow · launched July 2001.

Weblogs are personal news — democratic, timely, hard to find an audience. Blogdex amalgamates link structure across thousands of blogs into one navigable source: what the blogosphere is talking about today, ranked by how often blogs link it.

Why it matters in 2001: discovery leaves pure blogrolls and enters instrumentation. Pair with Movable Type, Blogger, and the personal filter of CamWorld-era link lists.
