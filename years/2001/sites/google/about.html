About Google

Google's mission is to organize the world's information, making it universally accessible and useful. Founded September 1998 by Larry Page and Sergey Brin. In June 2001 Google completed a $25 million equity round led by Sequoia Capital and Kleiner Perkins.

Technology highlights: PageRank, dynamic summaries, cached web, and the "I'm feeling lucky" button. No portal litter — and no ads on the home page (2001).

In June 2001, Netscape Search began using Google technology alongside the Open Directory. Still, Media Metrix search share lists are led by Yahoo! and MSN — Google grows by word of mouth.
