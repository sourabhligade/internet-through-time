Election online — traffic template

November–December 2000 Florida recounts and Supreme Court drama drove unprecedented traffic to news sites. Maps, chads, and live updates turned the Web into a second living room — jittery, partisan, and suddenly essential.

In 2001 that spike is the template: newsrooms staff always-on desks, readers expect minute-by-minute pages, and cable + web cross-promote. War coverage later in the year reuses the same habit.
