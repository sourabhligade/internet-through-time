AOL Time Warner

January 2001: America Online and Time Warner complete the merger era story that began in 2000. Press once called it proof old and new media would converge. For a few weeks it feels like a coronation. By spring, the NASDAQ hangover reframes the entire year.

On the ground: AOL brands still sell dial-up “You’ve Got Mail” culture while broadband and open-web tools (wiki, blogs, Google) quietly steal attention. CNN Interactive sits inside the merged company — newsrooms keep shipping section pages regardless of stock charts.
