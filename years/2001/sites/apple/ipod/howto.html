How it works

iPod · Tech specs · How it works · FAQ · iTunes 2

  1. Rip CDs in iTunes 2 on your Mac.
  2. Plug iPod via FireWire — auto-sync library + playlists.
  3. Unmount, pocket it, scroll wheel through 1,000 songs.

Scroll wheel browses by playlist, artist, or song. LCD shows six lines with white LED backlight.

Not a music store. iTunes Music Store = 2003. This is Mac-first jukebox + player.
