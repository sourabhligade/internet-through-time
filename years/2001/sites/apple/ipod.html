Presenting iPod. The first MP3 player to pack a mind-blowing 1,000 songs and a 10-hour battery into a stunning 6.5-ounce package you can literally take everywhere.

Pocket-sized (2.43 × 4.02 × 0.78 inches). Scroll wheel. FireWire. Mac-first.

iPod · Tech specs · How it works · FAQ · iTunes 2 · Apple
Super-fast FireWire auto-updating
When you first plug iPod into your Mac, all of your iTunes songs and playlists are automatically downloaded at blazing FireWire speed. Download an entire CD in less than 10 seconds — or 1,000 songs in under 10 minutes. iPod automatically charges whenever you're connected.
iTunes 2 included
Automatic synchronization keeps your iPod music and playlists up to date. Rip MP3s, create playlists, burn custom CDs — and now EQ, crossfade, and MP3 CD burning. Not a music store. The iTunes Music Store is still in the future (2003).
Take your MP3 collection for a spin
Browse by playlist, artist, or song with iPod's unique scroll wheel. High-output amplifier · CD-quality sound · headphones with Neodymium transducer magnets.
