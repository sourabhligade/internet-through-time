iPod

1,000 songs in your pocket.

White-and-chrome hard-drive player. Scroll wheel. FireWire. Mac-first.

iPod · Tech specs · How it works · FAQ · Apple

October 23, 2001: Apple unveils a pocket jukebox — not a phone, not a store (the iTunes Music Store is 2003). Rip CDs you own. Carry ~1,000 songs. Claim up to 10 hours of battery. Pair with iTunes on a Mac.

Why it matters in 2001
Napster's court battles remade music culture. The iPod is the legal, shiny counter-spell: your library, portable, paid-for or ripped from CDs you own.		 What it is not
Not Windows-first on day one. Not a phone. Not streaming-as-default. Not the later multi-colored minis or touch screens of other years.
