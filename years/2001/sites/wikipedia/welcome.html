Welcome, newcomers

Wikipedia launches in January 2001 as a public draft alongside Nupedia’s expert process. You can edit pages right now — UseModWiki grammar, not a modern Vector skin. Be bold. Preview if you can. Save when ready.

Nupedia remains the “more rigorous and serious” expert pipeline. Wikipedia is the fast, community draft that soon outgrows that model. Contrast with Encarta-style packaged encyclopedias: here the page is a living edit log.
