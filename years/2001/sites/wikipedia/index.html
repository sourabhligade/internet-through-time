the free encyclopedia that anyone can edit · launched January 15, 2001

Welcome to Wikipedia. We are writing a freely licensed encyclopedia in many languages — collaboratively, in the open, using wiki software. No splash page. No banner ads. Blue links and an Edit tab. Nupedia's expert process is careful and slow; Wikipedia is the public, fast draft.
