About the 1998 year

Thesis: Portal peak + browser war + commerce growth. Yahoo still owns the start-page mindshare; Google! is brand-new and sparse. Amazon sells books and music (no smile logo yet). eBay is the IPO-era personal trading community (black logo, not multicolor).

How to play

  • Yellow tour on the Starting Point — check off each stop (saved in this browser).
  • Directory buttons under the Address bar jump to signature rooms.
  • Address bar: type google, yahoo, amazon, ebay + Enter.
  • Toolbar: Back · Forward · Stop · Refresh · Home · Search · Favorites · History · Mail · Go.
  • Start menu: Settings (Preferences) · Find · Run (Open Location) · Shut Down (hub).
  • Cart, bids, and personalize toggles use localStorage only — no real money or mail.
