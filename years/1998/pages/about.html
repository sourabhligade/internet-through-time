About the 1998 year

This room reconstructs how the Web felt in mid–late 1998: Windows 98, Internet Explorer 4, loud portals, a tiny Google, Amazon selling music, and eBay going public.

What to do: follow the yellow tour on the Starting Point, or type site names in the Address bar (google, yahoo, amazon). Cart, bids, and tour progress stay in your browser only.

Sources: Wayback Machine (1998), Web Design Museum, Version Museum, Cybercultural essays, W3C, company histories.
