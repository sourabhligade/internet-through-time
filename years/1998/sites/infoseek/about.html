About Infoseek (1998)

Infoseek is one of the portal-war search brands fighting for the “start page.” By 1998 the product mixes a web index with channel pages, news, and Ultraseek-style enterprise licensing lore — still a household name alongside AltaVista, HotBot, and Excite.
