On ranking the Web

Academic web search in 1997–98 still looked like AltaVista-style keyword soup. The Page/Brin insight: use the graph of hyperlinks, not only the words on a page.

  1. A link from A→B is a citation of B
  2. Important pages cite important pages (recursive)
  3. Query results can be ordered by that importance score

The public Google! site (1998) is the demo; Stanford papers are the theory.
