Hotmail in 1998

Sabeer Bhatia and Jack Smith launched HoTMaiL in 1996 as free webmail. Microsoft bought the company in December 1997 for a reported ~$400 million in stock — one of the first huge webmail exits.

By 1998 the product still says “Hotmail” in the browser chrome while Microsoft folds operations toward MSN. Yahoo! Mail is the consumer rival; AOL still owns the “you’ve got mail” cultural brand.
