Internet Explorer 4.0
Browse the Web Like Never Before

Key Features
Active Desktop
Live web content on your Windows desktop. Stock tickers, news, weather, and your favorite web pages — always visible, always updated. Your desktop becomes a window to the Internet.
Channels
Subscribe to web content that's delivered automatically to your desktop. Choose from channels like Disney, PointCast, Warner Bros, MSNBC, and more. Content comes to you.
Explorer Bars
Search, Favorites, and History all available in a handy sidebar. Browse your favorites while viewing web pages. Integrated search powered by your choice of search engine.
Dynamic HTML
Interactive web pages that respond instantly without contacting the server. Animations, transitions, drag-and-drop, and dynamic content changes. The web comes alive with DHTML.
Security Zones
Customizable security for different web sites. Assign sites to zones — Internet, Local Intranet, Trusted Sites, or Restricted Sites — each with its own security level.
Outlook Express
Built-in email and newsgroups client. Read your mail and participate in Usenet newsgroups right from Internet Explorer. Supports POP3, SMTP, IMAP, NNTP, and HTML mail.
