Open Directory Project (DMOZ)

Live June 5, 1998 as Gnuhoo / NewHoo — a volunteer-edited web directory. Netscape acquires the project later in 1998 and brands it the Open Directory. Unlike Yahoo’s closed editors, ODP data is meant to be open for reuse.

Why it matters in 1998: human directories still beat pure search for many users; ODP is the “open” rival to Yahoo’s commercial catalog.
