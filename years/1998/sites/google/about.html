About Google!

Google is a new search engine from Stanford Ph.D. students Larry Page and Sergey Brin. The company incorporated in September 1998. This site is a public beta.

Instead of ranking pages only by keyword count, Google uses PageRank — how pages link to each other on the Web — to find useful results.

Most people in 1998 still start at a portal like Yahoo! or Excite. Google is the quiet newcomer.
