About

Jason Scott launched textfiles.com in May 1998 to keep BBS-era ASCII from disappearing. The files are 1980s–early-90s. The website is a 1998 object.

Source we had never used:textfiles.com. 1994 in this museum has the BBS ritual. This room is the archive launch.

Bans: no warez dumps, no ripped commercial.txt.
