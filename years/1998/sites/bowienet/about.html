BowieNet — About

Launched September 1, 1998. David Bowie’s ISP and fan community: dial-up access in North America, premium content elsewhere, message boards, chats, and album-era visual language. Period press pitched it as an artist owning the pipe — not just a homepage.

  • Recommended modem: 56k
  • Frames, GIFs, and early multimedia samples
  • Proto-social: karma, boards, “outside” album lore
