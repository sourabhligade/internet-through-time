Dispatch · late 1995

Windows 95 is on store shelves. Netscape went public. Internet Explorer arrived in a Plus! pack. Amazon sells books. AuctionWeb sells whatever you list. Signal is the short list of what that feels like on a 28.8 line.

  • Browser war: Navigator vs IE — your ISP still mails a CD either way
  • Directories (Yahoo!) vs crawlers (AltaVista) — both open in different tabs… if you have memory
  • Everyone wants a homepage; GeoCities hands out neighborhood addresses
  • Secure checkout is a button you trust carefully
