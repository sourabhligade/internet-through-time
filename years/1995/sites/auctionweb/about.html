About AuctionWeb

AuctionWeb is a place for person-to-person trading on the World Wide Web. List an item. Watch the bids. Arrange payment and shipping yourself.

Launched Labor Day weekend, 1995. Early sales include a broken laser pointer that went for $14.83 — proof that people will buy almost anything online.

Note: This is AuctionWeb (not a later marketplace brand redesign). Keep expectations low, HTML uglier, and trust experimental.
