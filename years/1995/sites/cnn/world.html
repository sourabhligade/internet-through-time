World

International desks are coming online. Coverage still favors short wire-style summaries — photos are expensive on dial-up, so many “world” packages are text with a single logo GIF.

  • Diplomacy & markets— first online because the wires already exist
  • Conflict updates— brief; fuller packages still belong on television
  • Elections abroad— headlines first, analysis later

CNN Interactive is cable news learning the Web. Expect the on-air brand, simpler pages, and links that sometimes say “more on TV.”
