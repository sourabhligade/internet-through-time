The Web in 2005

Web 2.0 is a business boom. YouTube makes uploading video easy. Google Maps and Ajax make pages feel like apps. Reddit and Digg turn the front page into a vote. Podcasts land in iTunes. MySpace and Flickr get bought. Facebook is still mostly for students.
