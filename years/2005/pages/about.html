About 2005

2005 is when Web 2.0 becomes a business boom — YouTube upload is the save, Maps / Reddit / Digg / Pandora / iTunes podcasts are leftover, Yahoo is still #1 visits, and Google does not own YouTube yet.

Scale (Internet Live Stats, June 2005): 64,780,617 websites (+26%) · 1,027,580,990 users · 16 users/site. Birthmarks YouTube + Reddit.

Netcraft June hostnames 64,808,485 · December 74,353,258 (Apache 69.97% / Microsoft 20.92%). Print June and December separately. Do not blend the two cells.

Yahoo is still #1 visits (6.20B). MySpace enters top 10 at #9 (462M). YouTube is not on the visits list.
