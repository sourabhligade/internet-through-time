Google’s mission: organize the world’s information and make it universally accessible and useful.

2004 is different: search is habit; IPO (Aug 19) makes Google a public company story; Gmail (Apr 1) is invite-only webmail with a 1 GB pitch (“Search, don’t sort” · conversations · no pop-up ads); AdSense funds the blogosphere.

Still mostly a sparse homepage. Portals (Yahoo, MSN) still own many start pages — but “just Google it” is already workplace English. Year-end (Cybercultural): Google vs Microsoft’s desktop-as-platform — “Anti-Web 2.0” contrast in period geek talk.

Also 2004: first Web 2.0 Conference (Oct) · “Web as Platform” · business/investor audience · network effects talk · eBay envy / data lock-in debates.

Not yet in 2004 defaults: Maps drag UI (2005) · Reader (2005) · YouTube (2005) · Chrome browser · open Gmail signup (2007).
