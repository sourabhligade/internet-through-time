About Facebook (2005)

February 4, 2004 — launched at Harvard as Thefacebook: an online directory that connects people through social networks at colleges. Your facebook is limited to your own school — search people, classes, friends’ friends, visualization of the social network.

Welcome to the Facebook (rename-era home, mid/late 2005 captures): still a college directory. Open to a lot of schools, but not everywhere yet. Oh, and we redesigned the site.

2005 year beats:
· May 2005 — Accel Partners invests $12.7 million (Series A class)
· August 2005 — facebook.com domain (~$200k lore) / drop the “The” rename era
· September 2005 — high school networks expand beyond colleges
· Still not open registration for the entire internet (Sep 26, 2006)
· No News Feed yet (News Feed is 2006)

You can use Facebook to: look up people at your school · see how people know each other · find people in your classes and groups. A Mark Zuckerberg production · © 2005.
