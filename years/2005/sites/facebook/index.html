Welcome to Facebook

An online directory that connects people through social networks at colleges and high schools.

Launched February 4, 2004 at Harvard as Thefacebook. By late 2005 the brand is Facebook, schools include high schools, and the product is still an exclusive campus graph — invite your network, not the open web.

You can:

  • Search for people at your school or network
  • Find out who are in your classes
  • Look up your friends' friends
  • See a visualization of your social network
