Web 2.0 Conference

Period verb. Empty / trap never writes.

October 2004 · San Francisco · first conference under the “Web 2.0” banner

Thesis (period mood): the Web as platform — business and investors meet the blogosphere, social software, and browser apps that feel like software, not just documents.

What people mean by “Web 2.0” here

  • Web as platform — services, APIs, always-on apps (not only static pages)
  • Social software — blogs, wikis, tags, MySpace/Friendster/Flickr graphs
  • User-generated content — publish yourself; the crowd indexes with tags
  • Lightweight business models — AdSense-era blogs · freemium webmail lore
