About Mashable (2005)

~July 2005 — Pete Cashmore’s Mashable appears as a social-media / Web 2.0 culture blog. It is part of the same startup-press wave as:

  • TechCrunch — June 2005 · “Tracking Web 2.0” (Arrington)
  • ProgrammableWeb — August 2005 · API directory culture
  • Read/WriteWeb (MacManus) — tipping-point year for Web 2.0 blogging

Contemporaneous context: Cybercultural’s year review notes Mashable around July and the sold-out Web 2.0 Conference in October as boom energy — not yet the social-media megabrand of later years.

Not yet: Twitter · iPhone apps · Instagram · modern responsive “viral” redesign · open Facebook as mass social default.
