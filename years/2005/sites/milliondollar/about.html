About the Million Dollar Homepage

August 26, 2005 — Alex Tew (Wiltshire, England) launches a novelty site: one million pixels sold at $1 each ($100 per 10×10 block), advertised on a single page.

The stunt raised on the order of $1,037,100 — pure mid-2000s web lore. Optional P2 room in this museum: not a “product platform,” but a 2005 cultural marker of boom-era attention and ad novelty.

Exhibit honesty: The pixel grid here is a reconstruction theater — no real ad sales, no real payments, no live buyer database.

Same boom year as YouTube · Google Maps · Web 2.0 Conference · Skype / eBay (Sep 12).
