February 2005: Google Maps shows a draggable, zoomable map in the browser — a poster child for what Jesse James Garrett names Ajax (Feb 18, 2005 essay).

No full page reloads to pan. Developers notice. Mashups (HousingMaps and friends) prove APIs are culture.

Requires JavaScript. Works best on a modern-enough browser — Firefox fans love it; IE6 still the mass default.
