Nupedia

Nupedia is a free encyclopedia project that emphasizes expert peer review before publication.

It is slower and smaller than Wikipedia. The wiki experiment (January 2001) exists partly because the expert pipeline could not scale.

Both projects share a free-knowledge goal; the methods differ.
