PRINCESS DIANA KILLED IN PARIS CAR CRASH
August 31, 1997
Web posted at: 6:45 a.m. EDT (1045 GMT)
[Associated Press photo]
Pont de l'Alma tunnel, Paris

(CNN) — Diana, Princess of Wales, was killed early Sunday morning in a high-speed car crash in the Pont de l'Alma tunnel in Paris. She was 36 years old.

Her companion Dodi Fayed, 42, son of Egyptian billionaire Mohamed Al Fayed, was also killed in the crash. The driver of their Mercedes-Benz S280, Henri Paul, the acting head of security at the Ritz Hotel, died at the scene. Bodyguard Trevor Rees-Jones survived with serious injuries.

French police say the car was traveling at high speed and was being pursued by paparazzi on motorcycles at the time of the crash. The vehicle struck a pillar in the tunnel at approximately 12:25 a.m. local time.

Diana was initially taken to the Pitié-Salpêtrière Hospital, where emergency surgery was performed. She was pronounced dead at 4:00 a.m. local time.

Web sites overwhelmed as millions seek information online
News sites worldwide struggled to handle the enormous surge in traffic following the announcement. ABCNews.com recorded its first 1 million page view day. CNN Interactive, BBC Online, and other major news sites experienced intermittent outages as users worldwide sought information on the tragedy.

Buckingham Palace released a statement: "The Queen and the Prince of Wales are deeply shocked and distressed by this terrible news."

British Prime Minister Tony Blair, visibly shaken, spoke outside his Sedgefield constituency church: "She was the People's Princess and that is how she will stay, how she will remain in our hearts and our memories forever."

Thousands of mourners have already begun gathering at the gates of Kensington Palace, Diana's London residence, leaving flowers, candles, and handwritten notes. The scene is being replicated at British embassies around the world.

Diana's funeral is expected to take place at Westminster Abbey. Details are being worked out between the Royal Family and the Spencer family.

The Princess of Wales was one of the most photographed women in the world. Her marriage to Prince Charles in 1981 was watched by an estimated 750 million television viewers. Their divorce was finalized in August 1996.

Diana was widely known for her charitable work, particularly her campaigns for AIDS awareness, the banning of landmines, and children's charities.
