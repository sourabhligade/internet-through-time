After Napster: free files meet the 99¢ store

Peer-to-peer networks such as KaZaA still dominate casual music trading. Apple’s iTunes Music Store (April 28) bets that convenience and legitimacy can sell songs for a dollar each — if the catalog is big enough and the Mac users show up first.

The original Napster service is gone; the brand memory remains. Labels watch both charts: downloads that pay, and downloads that don’t.
