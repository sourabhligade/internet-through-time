YouTube · About

YouTube makes uploading and sharing video trivial in a browser with Flash. Founded February 2005; public beta April 23 with Me at the zoo. By late 2005 traffic is exploding. Google acquires YouTube in 2006 — not this year.

Early prototypes briefly carried dating-site form fields; the product that stuck is Broadcast Yourself — video first. This immersion uses mid-2005 video-product framing.
