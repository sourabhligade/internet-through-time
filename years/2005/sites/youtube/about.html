About YouTube (2005)

Founding story runs through early 2005; the public beta moment visitors remember is April 23, 2005. Tagline energy: Broadcast Yourself.

The product is not a TV network — it is easy upload plus a Flash player page you can link. Cameras get cheaper, broadband spreads, and ordinary clips go public without a studio deal.
