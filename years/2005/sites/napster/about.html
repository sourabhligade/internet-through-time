About Napster · 2001

Napster is a real-time search for MP3 files on other users' computers. We index what people share — we do not host music files on Napster servers.

Created by Shawn Fanning (1999). The mass-growth monster year was 2000. In 2001 the story is the courtroom: injunction upheld on appeal (Feb); temporary shutdown era (Jul); users migrate toward decentralized networks (Gnutella and others).

MP3 Copyright Policy (summary)
Napster supports the rights of artists and copyright holders and seeks to comply with applicable laws. Report infringement via period policy contacts (theater).
