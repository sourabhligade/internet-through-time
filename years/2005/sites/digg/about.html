About digg

Digg is a social news site: users submit links and the crowd diggs (votes up) or buries them. Front-page placement is a reputation game for geeks — “an improved, more social Slashdot.”

December 5, 2004 — Digg goes public (Kevin Rose, Owen Byrne, Ron Gorodetzky, Jay Adelson class). The traffic explosion is mostly a 2005 story.

Digg/bury and submit work for real in this browser (). Seed headlines stay 2004-correct (Firefox · Gmail · Flickr · IPO) — not Maps/YouTube.
