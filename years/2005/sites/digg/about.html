About Digg

December 5, 2004 — Digg goes public. Kevin Rose’s social news site lets readers digg (promote) or bury stories. Comments and categories (technology, gaming…) structure the front page.

2005 is the rise year — traffic challenges Slashdot for the geek front page (Cybercultural Top-10 Web 2.0 moments). Digg v2 redesign energy and categories land mid-year.

July 1, 2005 — first episode of Diggnation, the tech humor video podcast hosted by Kevin Rose and Alex Albrecht. Among the most successful early video podcasts; part of the same culture as digg/bury on the front page.
