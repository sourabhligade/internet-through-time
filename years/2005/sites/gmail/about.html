About Gmail

Announced April 1, 2004 — many people thought it was an April Fools joke. Google press framed free search-based webmail with storage on a different scale than Hotmail/Yahoo of the day.

  • Search, don’t sort — find mail instead of filing forever
  • ~1 GB free class (period pitch; press also used “500k pages / eight billion bits” language)
  • Invite-only beta — scarcity / eBay invite lore
  • Conversation view · labels · JS-heavy “app-like” mail (the word Ajax as brand essay is 2005 — do not anachronize)

Primary press lineage: Google press “Google Gets the Message, Launches Gmail” (2004/04). Extract: docs/references/2005/wayback-extracts/gmail-googlepress-20040401.txt.
