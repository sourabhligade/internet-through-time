Mozilla is an open-source web browser, designed for standards compliance, performance and portability.

Mozilla 1.0 (September 2001) is the milestone release for testing. Download Win / Mac / Linux talkback builds. Nightly builds most weekdays.

Also: Bugzilla · Venkman JS debugger · MultiZilla tabbed extension · NPL/GPL/LGPL relicensing.

IE 6 still wins on real-world site compatibility and market share. Mozilla is promising — and still bloated as a suite. The slim browser that becomes Firefox is not finished branding in 2001.
