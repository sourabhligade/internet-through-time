About Flickr · Home · Explore · Groups · Tags

Flickr is photo sharing: free accounts, photostreams, tags, groups, comments, favorites. Period tagline energy: share pictures · browse everyone’s photos.

Launched February 10, 2004 from Ludicorp (Stewart Butterfield & Caterina Fake), pivoting energy from the Game Neverending project into photo sharing.

March 20, 2005 — Yahoo acquires Flickr / Ludicorp (Flickr Blog confirmation day; period press ~$22–25M). Yahoo ownership is a current 2005 fact, not a future ban. Flickr continues toward “1.0” with the same team — not a sudden merge into Yahoo Photos; the Flickr blog promised the community flavor stays and the API remains open.

Companion tag culture: del.icio.us social bookmarks (Yahoo buys del.icio.us December 9, 2005 — “fraternal twin” energy).
