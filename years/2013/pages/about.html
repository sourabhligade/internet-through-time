About 2013

2013 is when the mobile internet becomes the default cultural layer — and privacy becomes a mass story. Vine (6s) + Instagram Video (15s) invent short-form video. Snapchat Stories invents the 24-hour ephemeral day-feed. iOS 7 kills skeuomorphism. Touch ID ships. Snowden / PRISM force surveillance into mainstream news. Bitcoin’s first mainstream boom/bust/raid year — while Instagram Stories / Reels / TikTok / Reactions / Meta do not exist.
