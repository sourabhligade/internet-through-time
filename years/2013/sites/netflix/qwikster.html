Qwikster

Historical exhibit only. In September 2011 Netflix announced it would split DVD-by-mail into a separate brand — Qwikster — after the July price hike. Customers revolted. By ~October 10 the split was cancelled. The price hike largely remained.

Streaming stays Netflix. DVD mail almost got its own site and queues. For about three weeks the internet argued about a red envelope with a new name.
