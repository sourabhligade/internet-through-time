Social login wars, late 2009

Facebook Connect makes “Log in with Facebook” normal on partner sites. Google Friend Connect (public class Dec 4, 2009) is the OpenSocial-flavored counter-move: paste a snippet, get members/activities without building your own graph.

Earlier in 2009 Facebook limited some Friend Connect integrations — the lesson for the museum is competing identity stacks, not a clean monopoly yet. Beacon privacy fights (from 2007) still echo.

Sources class: Google Blog “Friend Connect now available” (Dec 2009) · period tech press · OpenSocial continuity. No real third-party OAuth in this reconstruction.
