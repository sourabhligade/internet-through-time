## 0. Thesis (locked draft)

**2014 is when “being online” as a mass cultural default hardens:** platform capital buys the chat layer and bets on VR; the open web has its worst named SSL bug and its first **billion-site** milestone; the phone gets **bigger** (and briefly bends in the news); Google publishes a design language that remakes product UI; and viral charity + true-crime podcasts prove social media can industrialize attention.

Themes:

| Theme | Period truth | Why museum cares |
|-------|----------------|------------------|
| Messaging empire | FB × WhatsApp ~$19B class | Chat is the new social graph |
| Open-web panic | Heartbleed CVE-2014-0160 | Security literacy for everyone |
| Scale | 968M June · **1B Sep** | First billion-site web |
| Bigger phone | iPhone 6 / 6 Plus · Pay · iOS 8 | Phablet mainstream · NFC pay |
| Hardware meme | Bendgate | Product flaw as culture |
| Design language | Material Design · Lollipop | Google’s post-Holo UI bible |
| Virality industrial | Ice Bucket · Serial | Challenge + podcast boom |
| Empire M&A | Oculus · Twitch · Alibaba IPO | Capital map expands |
| Ambient future | Echo announce | Voice assistant seed (ships mass later) |
| Desktop honesty | Win7 residual · Win10 TP only | Not yet free-upgrade mass |

**Culture framing (Ringer 2024 class):** 2014 as the year influencer virality, activism, and harassment culture (Gamergate) show the power — and harm — of mass platforms. Museum may densify carefully; do not glorify harassment.
