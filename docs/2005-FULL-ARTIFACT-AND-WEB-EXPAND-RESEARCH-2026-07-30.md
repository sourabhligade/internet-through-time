## 0. One-line thesis (locked)

**2005 is when Web 2.0 stops being a conference meme and becomes a business boom:** Silicon Valley M&A returns (Yahoo→Flickr, News Corp→MySpace, eBay→Skype, Yahoo→del.icio.us); **Ajax** gets a name (Feb 18) and a showcase (**Google Maps**, Feb 8); **YouTube** makes upload trivial; **Reddit** and **Digg** turn the front page into a vote; **iTunes** takes **podcasting** mainstream (Jun 28; >1M subs in two days); tech blogs (TechCrunch, Mashable, ProgrammableWeb) invent the startup beat. Facebook is **still not** the open internet — college/high-school gated. Shell: **Windows XP + IE 6**; cool bloggers use **Firefox 1.x**. Mood = creative, API-happy, RSS-obsessed, **pre-smartphone**.
