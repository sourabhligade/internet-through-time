About
What is
Daypop?
Daypop is a current events search
engine. Daypop crawls the living web at least once a day to bring you the latest
information relevant to your searches.
The living web is composed of sites
that update on a daily basis: newspapers, online magazines, and weblogs. Weblogs
are a new form of personal journalism. Think of them as opinion columns or slices
of life. Newspapers give you the international headlines and weblogs give you
both a subjective view of current events and a personal view of the author's
life.
Currently, Daypop indexes over 5800
of the best news sites and weblogs on the net every day. If you know of a site
that should be included in the Daypop index, feel free to submit
it .
Use Daypop to search for the
latest news, the newest movie reviews, sports results or the hottest memes.
