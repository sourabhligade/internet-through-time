KEY FACTS FOR EXHIBIT:
- June 2000: 6% of Internet users had home high-speed (≈3% of all Americans / ~6M people)
- Feb 2001: 13% of Internet users
- End 2001: 16% of Internet users
- May 2002 survey: **21% of all Internet users in America** have broadband at home
  = **24 million** people / **12% of all American adults**
- Callback survey broadband mix: **71% cable modem**, **27% DSL**, 2% satellite/fixed wireless
- Framing quote (Part 1 title): Internet transforms from a “sometimes” tool to an
  **“always-on information appliance”**
- On a typical day: 82% of broadband users do something online vs 58% dial-up
- Broadband users 3–5× more likely to download files/music, create content, share files
- Excite@Home bankruptcy end 2001; shut doors **February 2002** (failure lore)
