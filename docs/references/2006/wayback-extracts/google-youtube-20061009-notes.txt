# Google To Acquire YouTube for $1.65 Billion in Stock
# Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1288776/000119312506206884/dex991.htm
# Visited: 2026-07-30

MOUNTAIN VIEW, Calif., October 9, 2006 - Google Inc. announced it has agreed to
acquire YouTube for $1.65 billion in a stock-for-stock transaction.
Following the acquisition, YouTube will operate independently to preserve its
successful brand and passionate community.
