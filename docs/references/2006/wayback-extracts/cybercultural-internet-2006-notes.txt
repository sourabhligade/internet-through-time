## Locked beats
- Social web breakthrough: Twitter Jul · Facebook News Feed + open Sep · Google acquires YouTube Oct
- No smartphones, fat laptops
- 2005 = decentralized blogosphere + social software (Flickr, delicious)
- 2006 = big tech asserts platform power / "maturing" internet
- Cloud: S3 Mar · SQS Jul · EC2 Aug · Bezos BusinessWeek Nov
- Web office: Writely Mar · Google Apps for Your Domain Aug · Docs & Spreadsheets Oct
- UGC king: Digg definitive · surpasses Slashdot · power laws (~30% front page from ~10 people)
- Digg Sep algorithm "digging diversity" · top users backlash
- Reddit similar ambitions but much less popular than Digg
- RSS geeks over-index · Google Reader redesign late Sep · most people don't know RSS
- Time "You" Person of the Year · Lev Grossman Web 2.0 · stupidity and wisdom of crowds
- Later clear: "You" not in charge — FB/Google/Amazon platform power only beginning
