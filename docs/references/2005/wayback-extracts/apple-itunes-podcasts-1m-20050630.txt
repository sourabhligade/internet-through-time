PRESS RELEASE June 30, 2005
iTunes Podcast Subscriptions Top One Million in First Two Days

CUPERTINO—June 30, 2005—Apple announced that in just two days iTunes customers have subscribed to more than one million Podcasts from the new iTunes Podcast Directory. iTunes 4.9 has discover/subscribe/manage/listen built in; new episodes auto-delivered to computer and iPod.

Quotes:
- Adam Curry (PodShow): "listeners are voting with their ears" · subscriptions dramatically increased · predicts tens of millions of new listeners
- August Trometer (iPodderX): "Apple is like the Starbucks of Podcasting"
- Will Lewis (KCRW): "propelled Podcasting into the mainstream" · servers swamped · downloads increased tenfold

Features reconfirmed: Auto-Sync to iPod · Podcast menu · bookmarking · color Podcast artwork.
