Diggnation: tech humor video podcast co-hosted by Kevin Rose (Digg co-creator) and Alex Albrecht.
- First episode aired July 1, 2005
- Among most successful early video podcasts (Revision3 era culture)
- Producer suggested naming Digg site "Diggnation"; Rose chose Digg for site, Diggnation for podcast
