March 20, 2005 — Flickr Blog: "Yahoo actually does acquire Flickr"

Yahoo has made a definitive agreement to acquire Flickr and Ludicorp.

Community Q&A (period voice):
- Flickr continues path to Flickr 1.0 and beyond; same management/team; preserve community and flavor
- Not becoming "a bunch of suits"; Ludicrew DNA stays
- Not becoming Yahoo Photos; Yahoo Photos and Flickr remain separate for foreseeable future
- Yahoo ID optional later; existing login continues
- Flickr API remains open
- Free accounts get more storage/uploads; pro cheaper too

WSJ / secondary: acquisition reportedly ~$22–25 million (Delaney, WSJ March 20 2005 class).
