Alex Tew (21, Wiltshire UK) launches Million Dollar Homepage August 26, 2005.
- 1000×1000 pixel grid · $1 per pixel · min 10×10 blocks ($100)
- Buyers supply image + URL + tooltip
- Raised $1,037,100 (last 1000 pixels auctioned $38,100)
- Viral marketing / novelty internet commerce culture 2005
