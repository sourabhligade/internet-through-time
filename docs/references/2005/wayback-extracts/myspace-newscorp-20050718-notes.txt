Los Angeles, CA, July 18, 2005 — News Corporation signed definitive agreement to acquire Intermix Media, Inc. for approximately $580 million in cash ($12 per common share).

- Acquisition includes MySpace.com — "world's fastest-growing social networking portal"
- Intermix exercised option to acquire remaining 47% of MySpace it did not already own
- MySpace + Intermix's 30+ sites join newly formed Fox Interactive Media
- LA Times: Murdoch deal continues old-media rush for web ad dollars; expected close Q4; Chernin: triple web traffic into top-5 class from ~40s
- Period scale claim: ~16 million monthly users at acquisition (Wikipedia class)
