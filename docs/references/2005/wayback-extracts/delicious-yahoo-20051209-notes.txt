December 9, 2005: Yahoo acquires del.icio.us (Joshua Schachter).
- ~300,000 users at acquisition (TC)
- Price undisclosed; period guesses $15–35M; Schachter later said "definitely less" than $30M
- del.icio.us blog: "joined the Yahoo! family" · working with Yahoo Search team · "joining our fraternal twin Flickr"
- Founded 2003 Schachter/Gadjokov
