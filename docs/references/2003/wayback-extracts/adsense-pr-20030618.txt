Title: Google Expands Advertising Monetization Program for Websites
Subtitle: Google AdSense Enables Sites to Maximize Revenue Potential While Enhancing User Experience

Key points:
- New self-service option for Google AdSense
- Publishers serve text-based Google AdWords ads on content pages
- Google pays for clicks (CPC)
- Expands content targeting services announced March 2003 to broader high-quality websites
- Apply online: www.google.com/adsense
- Editorial review of applications
- Activate by cut-and-paste small piece of HTML into web pages or ad server
- Online performance reports
- English language websites worldwide
- Premium AdSense for >20 million page views/month (dedicated account)
- Premium examples: ABC.com, HowStuffWorks, IBS, Lycos Europe, NY Post Online,
  Reed Business Information, U.S. News & World Report

Sergey Brin quote:
"Google AdSense improves the overall web user experience by bringing relevant,
unobtrusive, text ads to web pages rather than disruptive, unrelated ads such
as pop-ups and animations... By providing website publishers with an effective
way to monetize content pages on their sites, Google AdSense strengthens the
long-term business viability of content creation on the web."
