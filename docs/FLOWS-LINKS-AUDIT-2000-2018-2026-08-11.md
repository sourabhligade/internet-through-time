## How to read a year

| Label | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Machine** | ≥2 pages or persist-across-reload · incomplete never writes · typed blob |
| **Wired** | REAL JS (name/from-to/seed) but still one room or thin body |
| **Plaque** | Checkboxes + save · literacy, not a product |
| **One-click** | Button writes `"1"` or saves with no required field |
| **Unwired** | Pages exist; the trail does not share state |
