## 4. Room densify kits

### Gmail (P0)
- Login / invite pitch (April Fools realness)  
- Inbox with labels sidebar theater  
- Compose theater  
- **1 GB** storage claim vs Hotmail/Yahoo MB era  
- No real SMTP/IMAP; localStorage messages only  
- Immersion: `gmail.js` (authenticity expects hooks `data-gmail-login`, `data-gmail-list`)

### Flickr (P0)
- Pink/blue Ludicorp era grammar (not modern Yahoo redesign)  
- Photostream · upload theater · tags / folksonomy copy  
- Groups / explore optional densify  
- Yahoo acquisition **footnote only** (2005)  
- Immersion: `flickr.js` · `data-flickr-stream`

### Thefacebook (P0)
- Brand: **Thefacebook** / thefacebook.com  
- Harvard · then other schools through 2004  
- Profile · friends · campus network gate  
- **Not** News Feed (2006) · not open signup  
- Immersion: `facebook.js` · profile hooks  
- Facemash: optional About footnote (2003) — not a product room

### Firefox 1.0 (P0)
- Download / features / what’s new  
- Tabs · popup blocking · phishing protection  
- vs IE6 default shell honesty  
- Nov 9 launch · Dec 15 NYT ad culture  
- Authenticity expects “1.0” framing on product page

### Continuity (from 2003)
- MySpace densify (mass social king of 2004 narrative)  
- Google search · Amazon smile · Yahoo · Wikipedia  
- iTunes Store · WordPress · LinkedIn · AdSense · Bloglines  
- Friendster still exists but losing buzz to MySpace  

### Home / About
Four pillars: (1) campus social seed Thefacebook (2) photo social Flickr (3) web apps Gmail (4) browser challenge Firefox + Google as public company.
