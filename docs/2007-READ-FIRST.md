## One-line thesis (locked)

**2007 is the year the phone becomes a real web browser you carry — while most people still live on the desktop Web 2.0 stack.** iPhone announce **Jan 9** · ship US **Jun 29** · **Safari only** · **no App Store**. Gmail opens **Feb 14**. Street View **May 29**. Facebook Platform **May 24**. Twitter breaks out at **SXSW**. YouTube is **Google-owned all year**. XP + IE 7 is the museum shell. Vista is retail, not the default desktop.
