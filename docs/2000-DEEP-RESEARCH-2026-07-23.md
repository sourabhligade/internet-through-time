## 0. One-paragraph thesis (verified)

**2000 is peak, crash, and cultural acceleration at once.** Y2K fizzles in January; **AOL–Time Warner** (~$165B, Jan 10–11) is sold as “Internet Triumph” on the NYT front page, then the tech market peaks **March 10** (NASDAQ ~5,000) and slowly deflates (~50% by EOY). Failure culture sites (Startupfailures, FuckedCompany lore) and **Pets.com** (Super Bowl sock puppet → Nov shutdown) become the crash mascot. Meanwhile the open web keeps growing: Live Stats **~17.09M websites** (+438% YoY) and ~**413M** users. **Amazon** ships the Turner Duckworth **smile** (A→Z arrow) and stretches category tabs to “tab insanity.” **Napster** goes mainstream monster (~10M May → tens of millions; RIAA injunction Jul 26; Fanning MTV + TIME covers) while **Gnutella** (Mar 14, Nullsoft) shows decentralized P2P. **Flash 5** (Aug) + splash pages + Homestar Runner define cool; **blogrolls** and social news (Slashdot Webbys, MetaFilter) make the web feel conversational. Browsers: **IE 5.5** (Jun/Jul) + **Windows ME** (Sep 14); **Netscape 6** (Nov 14, Gecko) ships late/buggy. **PayPal** lineage merges as **X.com + Confinity** (March). Mood = **bubble hangover + music culture war + Flash exuberance** — not yet Wikipedia, not XP, not streaming Napster.

## 3. Year grammar — how 2000 *feels*

### 3.1 OS / browser (target)

| Layer | Spec | Evidence |
|-------|------|----------|
| OS | Win98 SE most of year; ME optional late | WinME Sep 14 |
| Default browser | IE 5.5 (or 5.01 early) | WDM |
| Secondary | Netscape 6 (Nov) educational | WDM — buggy Gecko consumer ship |
| Modem | 56k home default; cable/DSL rising | Continuity + Napster campus lore |
| Design width | ~800×600 still assumed for mass commerce | LukeW Amazon note |

### 3.2 Design culture

- **Flash splash pages** and intro movies expected on “cool” sites.  
- Agency portfolio sites (2Advanced, GMUNK) = motion design porn.  
- Portals denser / stickier under crash pressure.  
- Google remains **anti-portal** sparse white.  
- Amazon = commercial maximalism (tabs + smile).  
- Failure blogs/sites = dark humor of the year.

### 3.3 Cultural dual state

Cybercultural closing line: financial euphoria gone, but web culture never more alive — Flash, blogs, social news, downloading.
