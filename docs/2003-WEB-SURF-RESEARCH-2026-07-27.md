### 1.1 Cybercultural — *What the Internet Was Like in 2003*

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| URL | https://cybercultural.com/p/internet-2003/ |
| Status | `[visited]` 2026-07-27 |
| Author framing | Richard MacManus · post-crash optimism · pre–Web 2.0 brand year |

**Thesis distilled from page body:**

- Dot-com worst is behind; entrepreneurs “coming out of hibernation.” Web 2.0 name/conference is still **2004**.  
- **Blogging + RSS** go mainstream (NetNewsWire, FeedDemon desktop; **Bloglines** browser mid-year).  
- **Google** assembles the puzzle: buys **Blogger** (Feb) · launches content ads (Mar) · **AdSense** self-serve (Jun) · funds pro blogs (Gawker path).  
- **Social networks** take recognizable form: **Friendster** public mass + **MySpace** seed.  
- **iTunes Music Store** makes legal downloads mainstream (99¢ AAC · Mac first · Windows Oct · 25M songs by Dec).  
- **Flash** is nearly “default cool” for creative sites (Jamiroquai, BowieNet v3, FWA / tokyoplastic).  
- Vocabulary: Clay Shirky **“social software”** (Release 1.0, May 2003) — WA link cited on page: `20031008062826` shirky.com group_politics.  
- Late-2003 footnote only: **Facemash** (Zuckerberg) — **not** Facebook product room.
