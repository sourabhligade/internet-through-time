## How the % is scored

One number per year. **Not** “HTML count / forest size.” A 28-page lean year can outscore a fat clone if flows and story are right.

| Layer | Weight | What “100” means |
|-------|-------:|------------------|
| **A Story** | 15 | Home + About thesis, scale (dual-cite when late), bans |
| **B Flows / densify** | 25 | Star + P0 REAL, incomplete writes nothing, trails, ~year-true rooms |
| **C Pixels** | 20 | Dated WA/evolt stills **or** labeled failed-final / continuity. Empty folder with no attempt = low. |
| **D Provenance** | 10 | CAPTURE-LOG / ASSETS match disk |
| **E Gates** | 20 | Hub card · check-all-years · year e2e (mvp/flows; densify+trail extra) |
| **F Isolation** | 10 | Year prefix `itt` / `itt95`…`itt11` only |

**Ceiling:** no year is 100%. Perfect WA logos and extra skins are residual forever and sit outside this score.

**Two readings**

- **Required bar %** = table below (A–F). This is “can we ship this year.”  
- **Feel vs peak** = how full the year feels next to 2005–2009 forests. Lean years lose here even when the bar is mostly green.

## What the numbers mean

### 1994–1999 (~91–94%)

Playable, gated, museum-ready claimed. Early web story is strong (FishCam, Yahoo, GeoCities, HoTMaiL, eBay, Google).  
**Left:** some years have no `densify.spec` / `trail-real-flows.spec` (1995–1999). OEM toolbar bitmaps and a few true WA crops are residual.

### 2000–2007 (~94–96%)

Densest years. Multipage P0, lots of period GIFs, full e2e packs. **2005 and 2007** are the feel peak (YouTube / iPhone / Gmail era).  
**Left:** residual WA logos, voice nits, not missing products.

### 2008–2009 (~93–94%)

Story and flows are museum-ready. Pixel **count** is low (15–17 files) because many brands reuse 2007 continuity — C is “honest,” not lush.  
**Left:** more year-native stills if you want the folder to look like 2005.

### 2010 — **80% required · 62% feel**

Lean from-scratch (~22 rooms). Star Instagram works. iPad / iPhone 4 / Open Graph / FarmVille peak / leftovers write `itt10-*`. About dual-cite + Pingdom table. e2e mvp + flows + densify + trail green.

**Why not higher**

- **C 48%:** `assets/period/2010/` exists but is **empty**. Rooms stamp `[failed-final]`. That closes the bar honestly; it does not *look* museum-grade.  
- **B 84%:** machines exist; many continuity rooms are still one paragraph. Not a 115-room forest (and should not be).  
- **D 72%:** CAPTURE rewritten; stills were never landed.

**To +10 points:** land 5 real WA stills (iPad, iPhone 4, instagr.am, Facebook bar, Foursquare continuity).

### 2011 — **81% required · 64% feel**

Same lean shape. Star Google+ + Spotify US + iPad 2 + Siri + Timeline densified from opened sources. 34/34 then 48/48 e2e including densify/trail.

**Why not higher:** same empty pixel folder as 2010. Continuity stubs (Chrome / IE9 / Android) are thin. YouTube play and Netflix 30% are in.

**To +10 points:** 5 WA stills (Spotify, G+, Timeline, iPad 2, 4S).

## Honest one-liners

- **1994–2009** are done enough to call **museum-ready**. Leftover is polish, not missing years.  
- **2010 and 2011** are **done as lean years** (~80% of the required bar). They are **not** as complete as 2007. The hole is **pixels**, not missing Instagram / Google+.  
- Nothing is 100%. The last 5–10% on old years is “prettier stills,” not broken flows.  
- **2012 is 0%** until you run the same research → lean implement process.
