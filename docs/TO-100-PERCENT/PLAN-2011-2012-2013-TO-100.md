## 1. Hard rules (every phase)

1. **Static museum only** — no backends, no real OAuth/CDN/maps/ride-hail.  
2. **localStorage theater only** — prefixes `itt11` · `itt12` · `itt13`.  
3. **Never invent brand pixels** — Wayback / Apple-Microsoft PR / press stills **or** RECON wordmark + CAPTURE `[failed-final]`.  
4. **Dual-cite scale** — label Live Stats **June** vs Netcraft/Pingdom **Dec**; never blend.  
5. **Hard bans stay hard** (year About + rooms):  
   - 2011: no IG Android as default, no Timeline everywhere earlier, etc.  
   - 2012: no Stories (Meta), no Meta branding, no Reactions, no TikTok  
   - 2013: no TikTok, no IG/FB Stories (Meta), no Reels, no Reactions, no iPhone 6 default, no Win10  
6. **Git only if asked.**  
7. **e2e after each year pass** — do not claim phase done without green gates.  
8. Work **one year at a time** inside a pass; finish that year’s phase before the next year for the same layer.
