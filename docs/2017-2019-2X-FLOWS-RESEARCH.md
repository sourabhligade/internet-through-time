**Date:** 2026-09-24  
**Status:** Research record. The trails are already wired. Do not add a 31st stop from this file.  
**Now:** each year has **10 official flows** and **20 leftover trail stops** (30 total) in `js/config/flow-trails.js`.

2× more means **20 leftover flows** on top of the official 10. Total **30 unique flows** per year. One dest, one verb, one key. A leftover flow is not a second key on an official dest.

---

## How the research runs

1. Freeze the official 10. Those slugs and keys stay.
2. A leftover candidate must be famous in **that** year, cited, and not already an official stop or a leftover-3× dest.
3. Prefer a page that is already on disk. Do not invent a site to fill the 20.
4. Empty never writes. The trap never writes. A finished visit writes `ittYY-<slug>` and never the year star.
5. Stop at 20. The other cited leftovers stay off the trail.
