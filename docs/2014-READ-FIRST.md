## 1. One-line thesis (locked)

**2014 is when platform capital + bigger phones + open-web panic industrialize the modern internet:** **Facebook buys WhatsApp** (announce **Feb 19** · **~$16B equity + $3B RSUs ≈ $19B press class** · close **Oct 6**) for the mobile messaging graph; **Heartbleed** (**CVE-2014-0160**, **Apr 7**) forces password-rotation literacy; the open web **first crosses 1 billion websites** (**Sep**, Live Stats + Netcraft Oct survey); **iPhone 6 / 6 Plus** + **iOS 8** + **Apple Pay** redefine the pocket computer while **Bendgate** becomes hardware-meme literacy; **Material Design / Android L** remakes product UI language; **Ice Bucket Challenge** and **Serial** prove viral video + podcast culture at industrial scale; **Twitch → Amazon**, **Oculus → Facebook**, **Alibaba IPO**, **Echo announce** expand empire maps — while **Instagram Stories / Reels / TikTok / FB Reactions / Meta branding do not exist**, **Apple Watch does not ship**, and **Windows 10 is Technical Preview only**.

### Mood (period voice)

> $19 billion for a chat app · change your passwords (again) · bigger phones that might bend · dump ice water for ALS · Serial every Thursday · Material green buttons · 1B websites on the news · Insider Preview, not Windows 10 retail · “Alexa?” is invitation-only · no Stories on Instagram yet.
