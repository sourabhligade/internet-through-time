## 1. One-line thesis (locked draft)

**2014 is when platform capital + bigger phones + open-web panic industrialize the modern internet:** **Facebook buys WhatsApp** (~$19B class, Feb) for mobile messaging empire; **Heartbleed** (CVE-2014-0160, Apr) forces password-rotation literacy on the open web; the open web **crosses 1 billion websites** (Sep, Netcraft/Live Stats); **iPhone 6 / 6 Plus** + **Apple Pay** + **iOS 8** redefine the pocket computer while **Bendgate** becomes meme hardware literacy; **Material Design / Android L** reshapes product UI language; **Ice Bucket Challenge** and **Serial** prove viral + podcast culture at industrial scale; **Twitch → Amazon**, **Oculus → Facebook**, **Alibaba IPO**, **Amazon Echo announce** expand empire maps — while **Instagram Stories / Reels / TikTok / FB Reactions / Meta branding do not exist**, **Apple Watch does not ship**, and **Windows 10 is Technical Preview only**.

### Mood (period voice)

> $19 billion for a chat app · change your passwords (again) · bigger phones that might bend · dump ice water for ALS · Serial every Thursday · Material green buttons · 1B websites on the news · Insider Preview, not Windows 10 retail · “Alexa?” is invitation-only rumor · no Stories on Instagram yet.
