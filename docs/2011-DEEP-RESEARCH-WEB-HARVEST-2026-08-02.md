## 0. One-line thesis (locked)

**2011 is the year the internet becomes a streaming + cloud + algorithmic-social stack:** **Spotify** finally opens the **United States** (Jul 14); **Netflix** is the loudest pipe on North American broadband then nearly dies of **Qwikster**; **Google+** forces Facebook into **Timeline** and the first mass **algorithmic News Feed**; **Siri** is the first widely felt consumer “AI assistant” on **iPhone 4S** with **iOS 5** and **iCloud**; **Steve Jobs dies** the next day; **iPad 2** proves the tablet wasn’t a one-off; **IE 9** tries to modernize the mass browser; **Snapchat** invents ephemeral mobile messaging as a seed; **Android 4.0 Ice Cream Sandwich** unifies phone and tablet UI — while the **desktop is still the mass default**, **Instagram is still iOS-only**, and **Uber is not yet UberX for everyone**.
