## 0. Thesis (verified 2026-07-26)

**2001 is the year the Web gains a memory and a legal portable jukebox under a solemn sky.**

| Pillar | What visitors should feel |
|--------|---------------------------|
| **Memory** | Wikipedia (anyone can edit) + public Wayback Machine (time travel for URLs) |
| **Music path** | Napster court death → iTunes library (rip/mix/burn) + iPod “1,000 songs in your pocket” — **no Store** |

| **Blogs grow up** | Linkblogs → warblogs after 9/11; Movable Type pro tools; Blogdex network; Weblogs.com pings |
| **Mood** | Dot-com hangover + post-9/11 seriousness + durable tools — **not** 2000 crash carnival, **not** 2002 always-on social |
