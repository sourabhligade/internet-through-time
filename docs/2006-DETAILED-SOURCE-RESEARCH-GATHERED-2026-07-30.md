## 3. Year narrative — Cybercultural “Internet 2006”

**Source:** https://cybercultural.com/p/internet-2006/ (Richard MacManus, Jun 28 2024)  
**Stored notes:** `references/2006/wayback-extracts/cybercultural-internet-2006-notes.txt`

### Locked narrative beats

1. **Social web breakthrough** — Facebook + YouTube ramp; Twitter born; UGC becomes king.  
2. **No smartphones** — fat laptops; desktop web.  
3. **Centralization begins** — if 2005 was decentralized blogosphere + social software (Flickr, delicious), 2006 is big tech asserting platform power (seen at the time as “maturing”).  
4. **Cloud computing** — Amazon S3 (Mar) · SQS · EC2 (Aug); Bezos BusinessWeek Nov “risky bet.”  
5. **Web office** — Google acquires Writely (Mar) · Google Apps for Your Domain (Aug class) · Google Docs & Spreadsheets (Oct).  
6. **UGC / Digg** — Digg definitive UGC site; surpasses Slashdot; power laws (~30% front page from ~10 people in MacManus research); Sep algorithm update backlash. Reddit similar ambitions but less popular.  
7. **RSS geek over-index** — Google Reader Sep redesign excites power users; most people still don’t know RSS.  
8. **Time “You”** — Person of the Year validates Web 2.0 culturally; Lev Grossman: harnesses stupidity *and* wisdom of crowds.  
9. **Platform power** — later clear that “You” were not in charge; FB/Google/Amazon only beginning to flex.

### Copy kit (period voice)

- “What are you doing?” (Twitter)  
- “News Feed” / “Mini-Feed” / privacy controls  
- “Broadcast Yourself” (YouTube) · now with Google deal lore late year  
- “digg it” / “bury” · power diggers  
- “Web 2.0” already feels like marketing hype to some bloggers  
- “the cloud” is developer/AWS language — not mass consumer brand yet
