## 0. One-line thesis

**2001 is the year the Web gains a memory and a portable jukebox while the bubble’s hangover and 9/11 make everything feel serious:** free collaborative encyclopedia (Wikipedia), public web archive (Wayback), legal-adjacent digital music (iTunes + iPod, still no store), Microsoft’s browser monopoly peak (IE6 + XP), and blogs that turn from linky journals into **warblogs** and professional tools (**Movable Type**). Sock puppets are gone; blue wiki links and white earbuds arrive.
