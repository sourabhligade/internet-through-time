# 2001 Research Dossier — The Internet Through Time

**Status:** Research + MVP scaffold ship 2026-07-23  
**Target:** Post-crash rebuild · **Windows XP** · **IE 6** · **Wikipedia** · **iPod** · Google as habit  
**Prior year:** 2000 (smile · Napster monster · Flash 5 · bubble deflation)

## Thesis

**2001 is the year the Web stops apologizing for the crash and starts building durable rituals:**
collaborative encyclopedias, portable jukeboxes, broadband as aspiration, blogs as daily media,
and search as muscle memory. The sock puppets are gone; the blue links remain.
