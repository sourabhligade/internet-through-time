## 0. One-line thesis

**2005 is when Web 2.0 stops being a conference meme and becomes a business boom:** Silicon Valley M&A returns (Yahoo→Flickr, News Corp→MySpace, eBay→Skype); **Ajax** gets a name (Jesse James Garrett, Feb) and a showcase (**Google Maps**); **YouTube** makes uploading video trivial; **Reddit** and **Digg** turn the front page into a vote; **TechCrunch / Mashable** invent the startup-press beat; **iTunes** takes **podcasting** mainstream (Jun 28). Facebook is still **not** the global social network — it renames from Thefacebook, expands to high schools, stays invite/network gated. Browsers: **IE6 still dominates Windows**; **Firefox 1.x** is the open-source choice of the blogger class. Mood = **creative, API-happy, RSS-obsessed, pre-smartphone** — not Twitter, not iPhone, not Chrome.
