## 0. One-paragraph thesis (verified)

**1999 is the year the browser war ends and two new rituals appear: peer-to-peer music and blogging.** Microsoft ships **IE 5.0** (Mar 18) into **Windows 98 SE** (Jun 10); by year-end IE holds roughly **75–80%** share while Netscape 5 never ships (Communicator 4.7 Sep; Mozilla milestones only). **AOL closes the Netscape acquisition** (Mar 17). **Google** is no longer a December-98 curiosity: **$25M** from Sequoia + Kleiner Perkins (Jun 7), **Netscape Search** licenses Google for ~60M pages (Jun 24), PC Magazine Technical Excellence, still **no ads** and still **not** in Media Metrix search-share tables (Yahoo ~46%, MSN ~40%). **Napster** launches mid-year (“music at Internet speed”; client **2.0 Beta 4** by Nov WA); RIAA sues **Dec 6**. **Blogger** (Pyra, Aug 23) makes reverse-chron publishing a form + FTP (“No muss. No fuss.”). **RSS 0.90** (Mar 15 Netscape) starts syndication wars. **Yahoo! GeoCities** is live branding post-acquisition (vanity URLs, 15MB, Ad Square vs GeoGuide). **Amazon** becomes multi-tab “Earth’s Biggest Selection” (Books, Music, DVD, Toys, Electronics, Home Improvement, **Auctions**, **zShops**, Sotheby’s). **eBay** is dense marketplace (~3M items; DE/AU/UK; Jun 10 outage lore). Scale: **~3.18M websites**, **~281M users** (Live Stats June 1999); landmark launch row lists **PayPal** (`19991013140707`). Culture: Matrix / Fight Club / Hampster Dance / Y2K / Bezos *Time* Person of the Year. Mood = **bubble peak + file-sharing culture war + millennium anxiety** — not yet the crash.

### 3.2 Design culture

- Portals denser than 1998 (Yahoo auctions + Messenger + GeoCities inside Yahoo).  
- Google **anti-portal**: white page, blue links, award blurb, no ads.  
- AltaVista = opposite extreme (shopping, free ISP, IE5 promo, media search).  
- Flash 4 enables richer (and noisier) motion + MP3 in SWF.  
- Tables + GIF still mass grammar; CSS/DHTML better in IE5.  
- Personal web: GeoCities under Yahoo + **blogs** as new reverse-chron form.
