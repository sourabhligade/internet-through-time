## 0. One-line thesis

**2006 is when the social web breaks through and platforms start to centralize:** **Twitter** is born (first tweet Mar 21 · public Jul 15); **Facebook** opens to everyone (Sep 26) and ships **News Feed** (Sep 5–6) — the feed that rewires the web; **Google acquires YouTube** for **$1.65B** (announced Oct 9 · closed Nov 13); **Digg** still beats Reddit for geek traffic and hits power-law / gaming drama; **Amazon** launches **S3 / EC2** (cloud computing for developers); **Google** builds a **web office** (Writely → Docs & Spreadsheets · Google Apps). *Time* names **“You”** Person of the Year for UGC. Mood = **platform power emerging**, UGC hype peak, RSS obsession among geeks, **no smartphones**, fat laptops — not iPhone, not Chrome, not App Store, not modern X.
