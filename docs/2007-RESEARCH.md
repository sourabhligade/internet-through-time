## One-line thesis

**2007 is the mobile phone breakthrough and open-web year:** the **iPhone** is announced and ships (still Safari-on-phone, **no App Store**); **Gmail opens to everyone**; **Google Street View** debuts; **Windows Vista** is retail; **Facebook Platform** turns Facebook into a developer platform; **Twitter** breaks out at SXSW; YouTube is Google-owned all year. Still pre-**Chrome** (2008) and pre-**App Store** (2008). Most people still live on the **desktop** Web 2.0 stack.
