## One-line thesis

**2008 is when the phone becomes a platform and the browser gets reinvented again:** the **App Store** ships with **iPhone 3G** (native apps, not Safari-only); **Android** reaches consumers (**T-Mobile G1**); **Google Chrome** launches as a web-app browser; **Firefox 3** Download Day peaks desktop indie culture; **Hulu** goes public and **Netflix streaming** densifies; **Spotify** launches in Europe (invite/ freemium seed). Desktop Web 2.0 still huge — but **gatekeepers** (app stores) and **Chrome** rewrite the map of who controls the client.
