## 0. One-line thesis

**2000 is the year the bubble peaks, pops, and the web culture keeps growing anyway:** AOL buys Time Warner (Jan) as the symbol of “Internet Triumph”; NASDAQ peaks **March 10** then deflates ~50% by year-end; **Pets.com** and failure-catalog sites become memes; **Amazon** finally ships the Turner Duckworth **smile** logo and stretches tabs to absurd multi-store chaos; **Napster** becomes a mass cultural monster (~10M → ~50M users) under RIAA/Metallica fire while **Gnutella** leaks decentralized P2P; **Flash 5 / ActionScript** and splash pages define cool design; **blogrolls** turn blogging social; **IE 5.5 + Windows ME** (late year) and still-dominant IE sit on most desktops while **Netscape 6** (Gecko, Nov) ships late and buggy; dial-up is still the home majority, with cable/DSL rising. The room should feel **louder Flash + crash anxiety + music war + everything-store**, not 2001 warblogs and not XP/IE6.

This year is **not** Web 2.0, **not** Wikipedia (Jan 2001), **not** Friendster/MySpace, **not** modern Google monopoly UI.
