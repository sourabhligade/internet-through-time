## Engines (full play, not Finish-first)

| Engine | How you play | Save |
|--------|--------------|------|
| `corridor` | Arrows strafe · click shoot · clear waves / reach the door | door or 3 kills |
| `gather` | Click nodes · spend on a unit · hit quota | quota |
| `platform` | Arrows + Space · 3 coins · spikes kill | 3 coins |
| `flap` | Click / Space climb · pass gates | 3 gates (score on crash if >0) |
| `fold` | Arrows or click an edge to merge | reach 16 |
| `match3` | Click two adjacent · match 3 | 5 matches |
| `cards` | Play 3 honest cards · trap card never scores | boss HP 0 |
| `craft` | Click a grid · fill the outline | outline complete |
| `rhythm` | Four lanes · D F J K or click | 8 hits |
| `solitaire` | Click red/black stacks · aces up | 4 aces |
| `idle` | Click bakery · buy one upgrade · reach 50 | 50 crumbs |
| `sling` | Drag back · release · hit the nest | hit |

`?test=1` / `?fast=1` after **Start** writes 12 so e2e can gate. Load / trap / no-Start **never** write.
