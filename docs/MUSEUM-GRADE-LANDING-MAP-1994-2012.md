## 0. Two bars (do not mix)

Same rule as the gap map.

| Bar | Meaning | Who has it today |
|-----|---------|------------------|
| **Museum-ready (A–F)** | Thesis + P0 rooms + honest pixels (WA **or** failed-final) + CAPTURE + e2e + `ittYY` prefix | **All 20 years 1994–2013** |
| **Museum-grade A** | One-thing **is** a gold product machine · leftover is **only L4 pixels** · forest does not drown the year | **1995 · 1996 · 1997 · 2005** |

**Gold product** = multipage or multipath · incomplete **never writes** · reload persist · period costume (not a checkbox plaque).

**L4** (never blocks A, never start here): perfect WA logo · evolt / GUIdebook OEM toolbar · modem WAV · live third-party APIs · dual-browser toggle · AOL walled garden · full Win8 immersive shell. See [`NON-DONE.md`](NON-DONE.md) N14–N19.

### How the percents are written

| Mark | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Ship %** | Share of A–F layers that pass. Failed-final C **counts as pass** (bar allows it). 100% ≠ “pixels look finished” |
| **Gold %** | Distance to museum-grade A. Letter map: **A = 100 · A− = 90 · B+ = 84 · B = 78** |
| **Feel %** | Lean years only. From [`2012-MUSEUM-GRADE.md`](2012-MUSEUM-GRADE.md): **~80% required / ~75% feel** after leftover close |

A year can be **100% ship** and **80% gold**. That is the lean contract.
