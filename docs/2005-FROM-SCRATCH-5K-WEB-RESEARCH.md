## 0. What “visit 5,000 websites” actually means

It is a **research envelope**, not a dest target. Same rule as Gray’s **10,022** (Dec 1994) and the 2025 5k pass.

The 2005 Web is **not** 5,000 sites. Internet Live Stats’ **June 2005** cell is **64,780,617** unique hostnames. Netcraft’s **December 2005** survey answered **74,353,258**. Around **75%** of later ILS hostnames are parked — do not treat hostname count as pages people used.

| Envelope | Count | What it is | Museum job |
|----------|------:|------------|------------|
| Gray Dec **1994** | **10,022** | Unique sites the Wanderer counted | 1994 scale fact |
| “5k websites” (this ask) | **~5,000** | A *walk budget*: notable + mass + year-true + product blogs | Open primaries. Curate **mass + signature + leftover band**. |
| ILS June **2005** | **64,780,617** | Unique hostnames · +26% · 16 users/site | Print as **June 2005** |
| ILS users 2005 | **1,027,580,990** | First ILS year users cross **1B** | People-online |
| Netcraft **Dec 2005** | **74,353,258** | Hostnames that answered that month | Print as **December** · dual-cite |
| ILS June table | **ends 2018** | Last June hostname cell | 2005 cell is legal. No 2019–2025 June. |
| Google Zeitgeist 2005 | MySpace #1 *gainer* | Busy web | Dual-cite mass. Yahoo still #1 visits. |

**This pass did not GET 5,000 live URLs.** That would be the wrong job. It opened scale surveys + year-true product posts + mass ranks + the entire local 2005 corpus, lined the **wiped** year against mass + year-true + bans, and wrote the leftover freeze.

**What this research is *not***

- A list of 5,000 URLs to implement.  
- A claim that hostname count = pages people used.  
- Adult top-10 entries as rooms. Name the rank. Do not reconstruct.  
- Baidu / Yandex as default US/English exhibit rooms.  
- Live video, live map tiles, or official YouTube / Google / Apple / News Corp pixels.  
- Google-owns-YouTube as a 2005 dest. That is **Oct 2006**.  
- Pandora as the star. That is leftover.  
- Dating-form YouTube as the gold UI. Honesty only.  
- A lean door. Size matches **2008 or more**.
