## 1. One-line thesis (locked — copy must match)

**2010 is the year the tablet and camera-phone app web arrive:** **iPad** invents a category; **iPhone 4** + **iOS 4** (FaceTime · multitasking · Retina) + **Antennagate** dominate phone culture; **Instagram** (Oct 6, **iOS-only**) invents filter photo sharing; **Facebook** hits **~600M**, ships **Open Graph** external Like, and becomes Hollywood; **Foursquare** check-in culture peaks; **FarmVille** peaks (~**84M MAU** Mar); **Android** (Nexus One) competes; **Uber** is SF black-car; **Pinterest** betas; **Wave** goes public and dies — while **Spotify stays Europe**, **Snapchat doesn’t exist**, and most people still live on a **PC**.

**Mood:** magical tablet · FaceTime grandma · death grip · Valencia filter · “I’m mayor” · Like on every news site · still fat laptops · Wave funeral.
