## 0. Thesis (verified)

**2002 = broadband always-on + blogosphere + post-Napster P2P chaos + IE6 monopoly + open-source browser rebirth.** Pew Jun 2002: ~21% of U.S. internet users (~24M adults) on home broadband. Design: MTV streaming portal + Flash microsites *and* Wired’s all-CSS Oct redesign. Social: blogrolls/trackback/RSS ecosystem named “blogosphere”; Friendster founds the friend-graph product category. Music: KaZaA ~100M downloads, Morpheus/Grokster chaos; iPod gen 2; no Music Store yet. Browsers: IE6 ~90%; Mozilla 1.0 Jun 5; Phoenix 0.1 Sep 23. Scale: **~38.76M sites**, **~663M users** (Live Stats June 2002).
