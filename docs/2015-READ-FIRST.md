## 1. One-line thesis (locked draft)

**2015 is when wearables ship, the free OS upgrade lands, and the phone becomes a live camera + music subscription + privacy toolkit:** **Apple Watch** finally ships (Apr 24, Sport from **$349**); **Windows 10** retail + **free upgrade** (Jul 29) + **Microsoft Edge**; **Apple Music** + **Beats 1** (Jun 30, after Taylor Swift royalty pushback); **Periscope / Meerkat / Facebook Live (celebs)** invent phone livestream culture; **Google Photos** unlimited high-quality backup (May 28 I/O); **iOS 9 content blockers** make Safari ad-blocking mainstream; **Discord** seeds gamer voice; **Let's Encrypt** public beta (Dec 3) free TLS; **Amazon Echo** goes mass retail — while **Instagram Stories / FB Reactions / Pokémon GO / Oculus CV1 mass ship / Messenger mass bots do not exist yet**, and the open web’s hostname count **dips −11%** after the 2014 1B spike.
