## One-line thesis (locked)

**2012 is the year the internet becomes visual and app-first, and Web 2.0’s cultural era ends** — Instagram ships **Android** (3 Apr) then Facebook buys it for **~$1B** (9 Apr); Facebook **IPOs** (18 May, $38, Nasdaq glitch) and hits **1B MAU** (4 Oct); **Pinterest** goes fully public (Aug); **YouTube** professionalizes “creators” and **Gangnam Style** is the first video to **1B views** (Dec); **iPhone 5 / Lightning / iOS 6 Maps** (12/19–21 Sep) and **iPad mini** (2 Nov); **Windows 8** retail (26 Oct) is late and controversial; **Chrome** overtakes **IE** (StatCounter, May class); **UberX** cheapens the black car (Jul) — while most people still live on a **Windows 7** laptop, and **Stories / Reels / Vine mass / iOS 7 / TikTok do not exist**.
