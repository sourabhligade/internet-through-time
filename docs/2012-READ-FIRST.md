## 1. One-line thesis (locked)

**2012 is when the internet becomes visual and app-first — and Web 2.0’s cultural era ends:** Instagram ships **Android** (Apr 3) then Facebook’s **~$1B** buy (Apr 9); Facebook **IPO** May 18 at **$38** (Nasdaq glitch) and **1B MAU** (Oct); **Pinterest** goes mass public; **YouTube creators** + **Gangnam Style** first video to **1B views** (Dec); **iPhone 5** / **Lightning** / **iOS 6 Maps** + **iPad mini**; **Windows 8** late and controversial; **Chrome** overtakes **IE** (StatCounter); **UberX** + **Snapchat** growth; the open web fights **SOPA/PIPA** (Wikipedia blackout **Jan 18**) — while **Stories / Reels / TikTok / iOS 7 flat / Meta branding do not exist**, and **desktop still matters**.

### Visitor story (why this year exists)

| Why | Explanation |
|-----|-------------|
| **Handoff from 2011** | 2011 opened Spotify US, Timeline, Siri. 2012 is when **Instagram leaves iOS-only**, **Facebook goes public**, and **apps become the default content format**. |
| **End of Web 2.0 culture** | Cybercultural class: “Web 2.0 quietly died” · mobile/visual internet born. |
| **Not only phones** | Desktop still exists (Win7 mass; Win8 controversial). Chrome’s rise is a **desktop browser** story too. |
| **Honesty over hype** | Win8 is not universal day-one. Apple Maps is broken. Instagram stays a **standalone brand** after sale (period promise). |

### Mood (period voice — use on product rooms)

> Finally Instagram on Android · “Facebook bought Instagram for a billion?!” · IPO day Nasdaq stuck · pin wedding boards · Lightning cable drawer · Apple Maps wrong lake · “where is Start?” · Chrome is #1 (StatCounter) · UberX cheaper · horse dance · Wikipedia went black for SOPA.
