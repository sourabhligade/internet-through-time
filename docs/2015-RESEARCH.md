## One-line thesis

**2015 is the year wearables ship, free Windows upgrades, phone livestreams, and unlimited photo backup redefine “being online”:** **Apple Watch** (Apr 24, Sport $349+); **Windows 10 free upgrade** + **Edge** (Jul 29); **Apple Music** + Beats 1 (Jun 30); **Periscope/Meerkat/FB Live celebs**; **Google Photos** unlimited HQ; **iOS 9 content blockers**; **Discord** · **Let's Encrypt** · **Echo mass** — while **IG Stories, Reactions, mass Messenger bots, Pokémon GO, and Oculus CV1 retail do not exist**, and website hostnames **dip −11%** after 2014’s 1B spike.
