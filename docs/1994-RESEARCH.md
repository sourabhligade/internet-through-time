## 0. One-line thesis

**1994 is the public Web’s first mass year:** Mosaic’s gray default gives way to **Netscape Navigator 1.0** (Dec 15); directories (Yahoo @ Stanford) beat full-text search; hobbyist sites, IUMA, NASA, and the White House share a **dial-up** experience at **14.4 kbps** (28.8 late year); there is **no production CSS**, HTML is still **2.0-era** with Netscape presentational extensions late year; tables are not yet a practical layout tool (that’s 1995).
