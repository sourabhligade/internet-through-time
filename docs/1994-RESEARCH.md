# 1994 Research Summary

See the project conversation / design dossier for full museum research.

Key facts used in the build:

- ~10,022 websites by Dec 1994 (Matthew Gray / MIT Wanderer)
- Dominant access: 14.4 kbps dial-up; 28.8 appears late 1994
- Mosaic early year → Netscape Navigator 1.0 Dec 15, 1994
- Default document background gray #C0C0C0
- No production CSS; HTML 2.0 era + Netscape presentational extensions late year
- Yahoo! at akebono.stanford.edu/yahoo (yahoo.com: Jan 1995)
- Tables not practical for layout until 1995
- GeoCities founded Nov 1994 as Beverly Hills Internet; aesthetic peaks later
