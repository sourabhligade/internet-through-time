## 0. Thesis (re-verified 2026-07-25)

**2002 = always-on broadband + named blogosphere + post-Napster P2P chaos + IE6 monopoly sleep + open-source browser rebirth + CSS standards moment.**

Pew (Jun 23 2002 report, May data): **21%** of U.S. internet users / **24M** adults on home broadband (was **6%** Jun 2000); among broadband **71% cable / 27% DSL**; always-on “information appliance”; **82%** broadband vs **58%** dial-up online on a typical day. Design: MTV streaming portal (tables + plugins) *and* Wired News **all-CSS** redesign (~Oct 10–11 2002, Bowman StopDesign). Social: Hiler “blogosphere” ecosystem; Daypop 6300→7500; Technorati Cosmos EOY; TrackBack (MT Jun 2002) + Pingback; Friendster **founded 2002** (public mass adoption often **Mar 2003** — see accuracy note). Music: KaZaA ~100M downloads; Morpheus FastTrack → Gnutella chaos (Register Mar 2002); iPod gen 2 Aug (Windows via MusicMatch); **no Music Store**. Browsers: IE6 ~90%; Mozilla 1.0 **Jun 5**; Phoenix 0.1 **Sep 23**. Scale: **38,760,373** sites; **662,663,600** users (Live Stats June 2002).
