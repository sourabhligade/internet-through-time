## 0. One-line thesis

**2008 is the year of app stores and browser reinvention:** Apple ships **iPhone 3G** + **App Store** (native apps); **Android** reaches pockets (**G1**); **Chrome** launches as a browser built for web apps; **Firefox 3** peaks indie desktop loyalty; streaming (Hulu public · Netflix densify · Spotify Europe) and social login (Facebook Connect · OpenSocial) reshape how people *arrive* at services. Still mostly a **PC web** year — but the **gatekeeper** story begins.
