## 0. One-line thesis (locked)

**2010 is the year the tablet + camera-phone app web arrives:** **iPad** invents a new device category; **iPhone 4** + **iOS 4** (FaceTime · multitasking · Retina) + **Antennagate** define smartphone drama; **Instagram** (Oct 6, iOS-only) invents filter-native photo sharing and hits **1M users** by year-end; **Facebook** hits **500–600M**, ships **Open Graph** / external **Like**, and goes Hollywood (*The Social Network*); **Foursquare** check-in culture peaks; **Android** (Nexus One · handset avalanche) competes hard; **FarmVille** hits peak MAU (~**84M** Mar); **Uber** leaves pure seed lore for **SF black-car** service; **Pinterest** beta launches; **Google Wave** goes public then dies; **WikiLeaks Cablegate** + **Stuxnet** put geopolitics on the web — while **Spotify stays Europe**, **Snapchat doesn’t exist yet**, and most people still own a **PC** (Win7 / IE8 / rising Chrome).
