## 0. One-line thesis

**2007 is the year the phone becomes a real web browser you carry — while most people still live on the desktop Web 2.0 stack:** Apple announces and ships the **iPhone** (Safari + multi-touch · **no App Store**); **Gmail opens to everyone** (Feb 14); **Google Street View** debuts; **Facebook Platform** turns the graph into a developer platform; **Twitter** breaks out at **SXSW**; YouTube is **Google-owned all year**; **Vista** is retail but **XP + IE** remain mass default; **Android** is announced (Nov) with **no consumer phones yet**. Mood = mobile *promise* + platform *power* + privacy backlash (Beacon) — still fat laptops for most of the world.
