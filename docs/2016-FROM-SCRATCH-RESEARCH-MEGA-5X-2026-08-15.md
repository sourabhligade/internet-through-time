|----------|--------------------------:|----------------------:|

| LinkedIn | **29%** | **25%** |
| Twitter | **24%** | **21%** |

More Pew locks:

- **86%** of Americans use the internet (footnote).
- **72%** of U.S. adults own a smartphone (at time of survey).
- Facebook **daily**: **76%** of FB users (55% several times/day) · up from 70% in 2015.
- Instagram daily **51%** of IG users · Twitter daily **42%**.
- Pinterest daily 25% · LinkedIn daily 18%.
- **56%** of online adults use **more than one** of the five platforms.
- Single-platform users: **88%** of them are Facebook-only.
- **93%** of Twitter users also use Facebook · **95%** of Instagram users also use Facebook.
- 18–29 Instagram: **59%** of online · 65+: **8%**.
- Women Instagram 38% vs men 26% of online.
- Online 65+ Facebook: **62%** (up 14 pts).
- LinkedIn: **50%** of online college grads vs **12%** HS or less.
- Auto-delete apps (Snapchat / Wickr): **24%** of smartphone owners (**+7** from 17% in 2015).
- 18–29 smartphone: **56%** use auto-delete apps.
- General messaging (WhatsApp / Kik): **29%** of smartphone owners.
- Anonymous chat (YikYak / Whisper): **5%** first ask.
- Mobile fact sheet class: U.S. adult internet **~92–95%** through 2016 waves · smartphone **~70–77%** (Apr 72% · Nov **77%**).
