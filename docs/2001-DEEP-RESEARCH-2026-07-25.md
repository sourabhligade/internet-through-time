## 0. Thesis (verified 2026-07-25)

**2001 = post-crash durable rituals under a solemn sky.** Wikipedia (Jan 15) and the public Wayback Machine (Oct) give the web a memory. Apple ships **iTunes 1.0** (Jan, library/rip only) and the **iPod** (Oct 23, “1,000 songs in your pocket,” Mac-first). **Napster** is dismantled in court (injunction upheld Feb; temporary shutdown Jul). **IE 6** (Aug 27) + **Windows XP** (Oct 25) lock Microsoft monopoly; Mozilla suite is promising but bloated. Blogging professionalizes: **warblogs** after 9/11, **Movable Type** launches **Oct 8**, Blogdex (Jul), Weblogs.com pings (Oct). Scale (Live Stats June 2001): **~29.25M websites**, **~500.6M users**.

Mood: ray-of-hope tech (wiki, iPod, blogs) inside bust + post-9/11 seriousness — **not** Friendster graph, **not** iTunes Store, **not** XP as “everyone already has it” in January.
