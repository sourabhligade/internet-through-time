## One-line thesis

**2010 is when tablets and camera-phone apps define the decade’s start:** **iPad** invents a category; **iPhone 4** + **iOS 4** (FaceTime · multitasking · Retina) + **Antennagate** dominate smartphone culture; **Instagram** (Oct 6, **iOS-only**) invents filter photo sharing; **Facebook** hits **~600M**, ships **Open Graph** external Like, and becomes a Hollywood story; **Foursquare** check-in culture peaks; **FarmVille** peaks; **Android** (Nexus One) competes; **Uber** is SF black-car service; **Pinterest** betas; **Wave** goes public and dies — while **Spotify stays Europe**, **Snapchat doesn’t exist**, and most people still live on a **PC**.
