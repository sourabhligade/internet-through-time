## 0. One-line thesis

**2006 is when the social web breaks through and platform power begins:** **Twitter/Twttr** is born; **Facebook** opens to everyone and ships **News Feed**; **Google buys YouTube** for **$1.65B** (late year); **Digg** is the UGC front-page king (with power-law drama); **Amazon** invents developer **cloud** (S3/EC2); **Google** builds a **web office** (Writely → Docs & Spreadsheets); *Time* names **“You”** Person of the Year. Still **pre-smartphone**, **XP + IE6** mass default, **IE7** late year, **Vista RTM** but **not** retail mass shell. Mood = platform centralization emerging after 2005’s decentralized Web 2.0 boom.
