## 2. Cybercultural — Internet 2005 (synthesis)

**Source:** Richard MacManus, *What the Internet Was Like in 2005* (Jun 1, 2024).

### Thesis bullets we adopt
1. Web 2.0 as **platform + business boom**, not just conference slogan.  
2. **M&A returns:** Bloglines→Ask (Feb), **Flickr→Yahoo (Mar)**, **MySpace→News Corp (Jul)**, **Skype→eBay (Sep)**, **del.icio.us→Yahoo (Dec)**; Google→Android (Jul, unnoticed).  
3. **Launches:** Google Maps (Feb), Reddit (Jun), YouTube beta (May/Apr class).  
4. **Ajax** named after Maps/Gmail inspiration (Garrett essay).  
5. **Tech blogs tip:** TechCrunch (Jun), Mashable (~Jul), ProgrammableWeb (Aug); RWW redesign end-year.  
6. **RSS over-estimated** culturally; algorithmic social wins later — but 2005 is peak RSS geek hope.  
7. **Pre-social-media mass:** Facebook still students; pre-smartphone; pre-consumer “cloud.”  
8. **IE still dominates**; cool bloggers on **Firefox 1**.  
9. Blog aesthetics: **sidebars, blogrolls, Flickr badges**, individual design (MT/WP).

### Exhibit implications
- Home page copy should say **boom / acquisitions / video / maps / vote sites**, not “social media apps.”  
- MySpace room needs **News Corp** beat without killing profile theater.  
- Flickr room should note **Yahoo ownership** after March.  
- Optional **Bloglines** or RSS about page supports thesis without full reader app.
