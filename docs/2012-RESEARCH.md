## One-line thesis

**2012 is the year the internet becomes visual and app-first, and Web 2.0’s cultural era ends:** **Instagram Android** (3 Apr) then Facebook **~$1B** (9 Apr); Facebook **IPO** (18 May, $38, Nasdaq glitch) and **1B MAU** (4 Oct); **Pinterest** fully public (Aug); **YouTube** creators + **Gangnam Style** first video to **1B views** (Dec); **iPhone 5 / Lightning / iOS 6 Maps** and **iPad mini $329**; **Windows 8** (26 Oct) late and controversial; **Chrome** overtakes **IE** (StatCounter) — while most people still live on a **Windows 7** laptop, and **Stories / Reels / Vine mass / iOS 7 / TikTok do not exist**.
