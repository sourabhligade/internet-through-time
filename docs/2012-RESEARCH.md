## One-line thesis

**2012 is when the internet pivots hard to mobile + visual media — and the Web 2.0 era quietly ends:** **Instagram** ships **Android** then is bought by **Facebook for ~$1B** (Apr); **Facebook IPOs** (May 18) and hits **1 billion** monthly users (Oct); **Pinterest** goes mass public; **YouTube** professionalizes “creators” and **Gangnam Style** becomes the first video to **1B views**; **iPhone 5** + **Lightning** + **iOS 6** (Apple Maps controversy) and **iPad mini** redefine Apple hardware; **Windows 8** Metro + **IE 10** reimagine the PC (late year); **Chrome** overtakes **IE** in global share (StatCounter); **UberX** cheapens rides; **Snapchat** explodes among teens — while **Stories/Reels don’t exist**, **TikTok doesn’t exist**, and desktop still matters even as apps eat the open web.
