## V3 — Cybercultural · What the Internet Was Like in 2012

- **URL:** https://cybercultural.com/p/internet-2012/  
- **Opened:** 2026-08-17 · author Richard MacManus · dated **5 Feb 2025**  
- **What it is:** Year-essay spine (same series as 2010 / 2011 harvest). RWW-era synthesis, not a 2012 primary screenshot.

**Minute extract**

- Dek: Internet culture pivots to **images and videos** — Instagram, Pinterest, YouTube creators, pictures on Reddit.  
- Lead: If 2012 was the year **Web 2.0 quietly died**, it was also when a more **mobile and visual** internet was born.  
- Meeker D10 (end of May): mobile “still in the early stages” but the biggest growth area. Android smartphone growth eclipses iPhone and iPad. Apps usurp web pages.  
- Instagram: “didn’t even have a functional website.” iPhone-only until **April 2012**, then Android, then Facebook “a matter of days later.”
