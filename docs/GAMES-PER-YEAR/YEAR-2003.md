## 2. How it works

### 2.1 Combat model

- Player HP 30 · Enemy HP 30.  
- 3 rounds max; if both alive after 3, higher HP wins (tie = draw).  
- Each turn: pick one gag:

| Gag | Hit chance | Damage | Special |
|-----|------------|--------|---------|
| Pie | 80% | 6 | — |
| Seltzer | 65% | 4 | If hit, enemy −10% accuracy next |
| Anvil | 45% | 12 | — |
| Cupcake (heal) | 100% | 0 | Heal 5 (once per fight) |

Enemy AI: weighted random; prefer anvil if player HP <12.

### 2.2 Turn flow

```
CHOOSE → RESOLVE_PLAYER → (anim 600ms) → RESOLVE_ENEMY → (anim)
  → check KO → next round or END
```

Text log: `You throw a pie! Hit for 6!` / `Miss!`

### 2.3 Presentation

- Two big face sprites (CSS emoji or div faces).  
- HP bars.  
- Gag buttons.  
- “Playground battleground” title.
