## 0. One-line thesis

**2005 is when Web 2.0 stops being a conference slogan and becomes a business boom:** Ajax gets a name and a showcase (**Google Maps**); **YouTube** makes uploading video trivial; **Reddit** and **Digg** turn the homepage into a vote; **M&A returns** (Yahoo→Flickr, News Corp→MySpace, eBay→Skype, Yahoo→del.icio.us); **iTunes** takes **podcasting** mainstream; tech blogs (**TechCrunch**, **Mashable**, **ProgrammableWeb**) invent the startup-press beat. Facebook is **still not** the open social web. Browsers: **IE6 on XP** is the mass default; **Firefox 1.x** is what cool bloggers use. Mood = **creative, API-happy, RSS-obsessed, pre-smartphone** — not Twitter, not iPhone, not Chrome, not “Google owns YouTube.”
