## Thesis
**2003 is when social + paid digital music + self-hosted blogs + professional networking go mainstream:** MySpace (Aug 1) personalizes profiles and music; iTunes Store (Apr 28) sells 99¢ tracks legally; WordPress (May) democratizes self-hosted blogging; LinkedIn (May 5) targets careers; Google AdSense funds pro blogs. Post-crash recovery energy. Not Facebook (2004), not Firefox 1.0 final (2004).
