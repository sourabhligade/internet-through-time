## 0. One-line thesis

**2003 is when social graphs, paid downloads, self-hosted blogs, and pro-blog ads go product-mainstream:** Friendster hits mass public scale; **MySpace** launches as the HTML-customizable fun alternative; **iTunes Music Store** sells **99¢** AAC tracks (Mac first, then Windows); **WordPress** forks b2 into open self-hosting; **LinkedIn** opens the career graph; **AdSense** funds professional blogs after Google buys **Blogger**. Mood = post-crash optimism + social software — **not** Facebook, **not** Gmail, **not** Firefox 1.0, **not** YouTube.
