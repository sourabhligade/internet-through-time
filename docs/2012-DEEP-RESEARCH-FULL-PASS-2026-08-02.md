## 0. One-line thesis (locked)

**2012 is when the internet becomes visual and app-first — and Web 2.0’s cultural era ends:** **Instagram** ships **Android** then is bought by **Facebook for ~$1B** within days; Facebook **IPOs** (May 18, $38, Nasdaq glitch) then hits **1 billion** MAU (Oct); **Pinterest** goes fully public and joins the top-50 web; **YouTube** professionalizes **creators** and **Gangnam Style** becomes the first video to **1B views**; **iPhone 5** / **Lightning** / **iOS 6 Maps** and **iPad mini** redefine Apple hardware; **Windows 8** reimagines the PC (late, controversial); **Chrome** takes the StatCounter global crown from IE; **UberX** and **Snapchat** accelerate the next decade; the open web also fights **SOPA/PIPA** (Wikipedia blackout Jan 18) — while **Stories/Reels/TikTok do not exist**, **iOS is still skeuomorphic (not flat 7)**, and **desktop still matters**.
