# Part 1 — Overall goals

## 1.1 One-line goal

Build a **museum-grade 2011 Web immersion**: **Windows 7 + Internet Explorer 9** shell (IE 8 residual honest; Chrome / Firefox as product rooms, not forced sole shell), period sites, and **real local interactions** that recreate how people used the internet in calendar year **2011** — especially **Spotify US (Jul 14)**, **Facebook Timeline + the start of the algorithmic News Feed**, **Google+ (Circles · Hangouts · +1)**, **iPhone 4S + Siri + iOS 5 + iCloud**, **iPad 2**, and **Netflix streaming peak + price hike + Qwikster** — while **Instagram stays iOS-only**, **Snapchat is only a late-year seed**, **Uber is not UberX mass**, and most people still live on a **PC**.

## 1.2 Why this year exists in the museum (visitor story)

| Why | Explanation |
|-----|-------------|
| **Handoff from 2010** | 2010 invented the tablet and Instagram-on-iPhone. 2011 is when **streaming music hits the US**, **feeds stop being pure chronological**, **voice assistants** appear, and **Netflix almost kills itself** with branding. |
| **Not just “more apps”** | The visitor must feel **cloud sync** (iCloud/Dropbox), **pipe-level video** (Netflix traffic), and **social redesign war** (Google+ vs Timeline). |
| **Still a PC year** | If the shell pretends everyone lived only on phones, immersion fails. Win7 + IE9 (or IE8 residual) is the mass desk. |
| **Honesty over hype** | Google+ was hyped and under-engaged. Qwikster was reversed. Snapchat was not mass. Instagram had no Android. Document that. |
