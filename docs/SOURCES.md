## 14. Legal & attribution stance

1. **Educational / historical reconstruction** — no affiliation with Netscape, Yahoo, Amazon, Microsoft, Warner Bros., eBay, etc.  
2. **Trademarks** appear only to illustrate period history.  
3. **Direct harvests** (e.g. Space Jam GIFs from the still-live 1996 promo) should remain attributed to their historical publishers; used here for museum reconstruction.  
4. **Reconstructed GIFs** (many logos/chrome) are inspired by public screenshots and period descriptions — not claimed as official brand packages.  
5. **User data** in the exhibit is localStorage only (cart, mail, tour) — nothing uploaded to these sources.
