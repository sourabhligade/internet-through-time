## Key Facts
- ~1,117,000 websites (web crosses 1 million)
- ~70 million internet users worldwide
- Browser war climax: IE4 (~35-40%) vs Communicator 4 (~55-62%)
- 56k modems (x2/K56flex) — upgrade from 28.8
- HTML 3.2 → HTML 4.0 (Dec 18)
- DHTML year; Flash 2; CSS1 partially used; push technology hype
