## 0. One-line thesis (confirmed by sources)

**2005 is when Web 2.0 becomes a business boom:** Google Maps + the **Ajax** name (Jesse James Garrett, Feb 18) · **YouTube** video upload culture · **Reddit** + **Digg rise** social news · **iTunes 4.9 podcasting** (Jun 28) · M&A wave (Flickr→Yahoo Mar, MySpace→News Corp Jul, Skype→eBay Sep, del.icio.us→Yahoo Dec) · IE6 still mass Windows default · Firefox 1.x for cool bloggers · pre-smartphone · pre-consumer “cloud” · **not** Twitter, **not** open Facebook, **not** Google-owns-YouTube, **not** Chrome, **not** iPhone.

## 3. Year narrative — Cybercultural “Internet 2005”

**URL:** https://cybercultural.com/p/internet-2005/  
**Author:** Richard MacManus · June 1, 2024  
**Stored:** `cybercultural-internet-2005.txt`  
**Status:** Full re-read 2026-07-30  

### 3.1 Opening thesis (key lines)

- By 2005, **Web 2.0 — the Web as platform** — was the driving Silicon Valley trend.  
- New tech bubble · startup launches galore.  
- **YouTube** and **Reddit** among future all-star launches.  
- Acquisitions: **MySpace → News Corp**; technological advances in RSS, mashups/APIs, **Ajax**.  

### 3.2 M&A timeline (from narrative)

| Month | Deal | Exhibit use |
|-------|------|-------------|
| **Feb** | Ask → **Bloglines** | RSS reader culture · “geek acquisition” |
| **Mar** | Yahoo → **Flickr** | Continuity Flickr must say **Yahoo-owned** |
| **Jul** | Google → **Android** | Quiet; optional about footnote only |
| **Jul** | News Corp → **MySpace** | Leading social sold; success-run lore ends |
| **Sep** | eBay → **Skype** | Multi-billion Web 2.0 deal class |
| **Dec** | Yahoo → **del.icio.us** | Social bookmarks buyer (Top-10) |

### 3.3 Launches

| Month | Product | Exhibit |
|-------|---------|---------|
| **Feb** | **Google Maps** | Mashups / API culture · Ajax showcase |
| **May/Apr class** | **YouTube beta** | P0 video |
| **Jun** | **Reddit** | P0 social news |

### 3.4 Ajax milestone

- Essay inspired by **Google Maps** launch.  
- Jesse James Garrett / Adaptive Path: *Ajax: A New Approach to Web Applications*.  
- Partial updates without full page reload.  
- Garrett notes Google products (Gmail, Maps), **Flickr** Ajax features, Amazon A9.  
- Ajax + RSS + APIs + Ruby on Rails = early Web 2.0 tech conversation.

### 3.5 Tech blogs tip

- **TechCrunch** (Jun) · **Mashable** (~Jul) · **ProgrammableWeb** (Aug).  
- RWW redesign end-year “RWW red”.  
- Technorati EOY 2005: TechCrunch ~#70 (Web 2.0 blog still early in ranking).

### 3.6 RSS over-estimated

- Bloggers obsessed with RSS; later algorithmic social feeds win.  
- Exception: **podcasting** keeps RSS.  
- Yahoo integrates RSS into products through 2005.

### 3.7 Pre-social-media / pre-smartphone / pre-cloud

- Facebook still **students only**.  
- You weren’t always online.  
- **IE still dominates**; cool bloggers on **Firefox 1**.  
- Blog aesthetics: sidebars, blogrolls, Flickr badges, individual MT/WP design.

### 3.8 Exhibit implications

- Home/About: **boom / acquisitions / video / maps / vote sites** — not “social media apps.”  
- MySpace: **News Corp** beat without killing profile theater.  
- Flickr: **Yahoo ownership after March**.  
- Optional Bloglines/RSS supports thesis.
