```html
<div data-4x-panel data-4x-kind="query" data-4x-min="2">
  <label>Period verb (not “leftover note”)</label>
  <input data-4x-field maxlength="80">
  <button type="button" data-4x-go="SUFFIX">Do the period thing</button>
  <p data-4x-status></p>
  <p hidden data-next-flow data-next-when-key="ittYY-SUFFIX">
    <b>Next:</b> <a href="RELATIVE">Verb · live dest</a>
  </p>
</div>
```

| Kind | Incomplete (never writes) | Writes |
|------|---------------------------|--------|
| `query` | field shorter than `data-4x-min` (default 2) | `{multiStep, real, year, ts, q}` |
| `checks` | not every `[data-4x-req]` ticked | `{…, checks}` |
| `hops` | fewer than `min` `[data-4x-hop]` clicks | `{…, hops[]}` |
| `wait` | skip timer | `{…, waited:true}` |
| `toggle` | not off **and** on | `{…, toggled:true}` |

Key: `ittYY-<suffix>` via `YearExtras.forYear`. Next dest is **this year’s** gold or the next leftover room — never only `pages/home.html`. Neighbor prefix never appears.
