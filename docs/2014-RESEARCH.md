## One-line thesis

**2014 is the year platform money, bigger phones, and open-web panic crystallize “being online” as we know it:** Facebook’s **WhatsApp** deal (~$19B class); **Heartbleed** password panic; the web’s **first 1 billion websites**; **iPhone 6 / 6 Plus** + **Apple Pay** + **iOS 8** (Watch announced, ships 2015); **Material Design**; **Ice Bucket** virality and **Serial** podcast boom; **Twitch/Amazon**, **Oculus/Facebook**, **Alibaba IPO**, **Echo announce** — while **IG Stories, Reactions, TikTok, Meta, Watch retail, and Windows 10 mass OS do not exist**.
