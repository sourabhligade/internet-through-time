## Why this exists

This pack is **14 years** (2010–2023). That is the largest single-band build in the museum. These are the AI-stack years. They must be **bigger and more complex** than a lean 28-HTML door, and their **flows and links must be 3× the current lean years** — not 3× the 2008 forest.

| Knob | Typical lean AI door now | **3× target** | Not this |
|------|--------------------------|---------------|----------|
| Dest rooms | 15–24 | **45–60** | 105 like 2008 |
| Content HTML | 27–58 | **90–120** | 345 like 2008 |
| Completable REAL flows | ~15–20 | **45–60** | 10,000 dest folders |
| Leftover dests | 9 (L2+L3+L4) | **27** (L2–L7, unique slugs) | clone L3=L4 |
| 3×-also | 80–94% dests | **100% dests · ≥3 live exits** | lobby paste on lean homes |
| Official 10 | often load-only | **10 finishable writers** | dest-field checkboxes as gold |
| Guided `<ol>` | 6 | **stays 6** | 7th step |

**Complex** = prompt · subscribe · generate · share · waitlist · leave. Incomplete never writes. No invented brand pixels.

**10k websites** = research envelope (Gray **10,022** Dec 1994 → ILS June through 2018 → Netcraft January after). Not 10,000 GET-and-scrape this turn. Not 10,000 folders.

## Shared laws (every year, every flow)

1. Incomplete REAL **never writes**. Load, empty Finish, trap, score 0 = no `localStorage`.
2. Prefix **`ittYY-*` only**. Neighbor year prefix absent after a write.
3. **Never invent brand pixels.** No official ChatGPT / GPT / Discord / Fortnite / Disney / Meta marks.
4. Guided home `<ol>` = **exactly 6**. Chip on playable + residual, not a 7th step.
5. Star dest and key **do not move**.
6. Traps never write.
7. After new rooms: `python3 scripts/build-3x-links.py` (idempotent).
8. ILS June websites table **ends 2018**. After that: Netcraft **January** + ITU people-online, labeled.
9. Lean first door (~50 HTML) **then** 3× densify. Do not start at 120 HTML.
10. Do not restore 2005–2007 in this pack.
11. Do not implement all 14 years in one pass. Name one year.

## 10k visit protocol (research envelope, not dest count)

### What “visit 10k websites” means here

Matthew Gray’s Wanderer counted **10,022 unique hostnames in December 1994**. Internet Live Stats reprints the June spine through **2018** (`1,630,322,579`). After that the museum prints **Netcraft January** + **ITU people-online**. Hostname count is not “pages people used” and is not a folder target.

Primary cites: [Gray web-growth](https://stuff.mit.edu/people/mkgray/net/web-growth-summary.html) · [ILS total websites](https://www.internetlivestats.com/total-number-of-websites/).

This corpus **does not pretend** we opened 10,000 live URLs in this turn. It folds:

1. Every dest already on disk 2010–2022 (selectors + keys + incomplete/complete/trap).
2. Official 10 + leftover L2–L7 + popular F + leftover-official + playables.
3. URLs already recorded in year research / harvest / 10k docs.
4. 2023 dual-cites opened for the lock.

When a year is named for densify, open **that year’s** recorded URL list below + official newsrooms before adding a dest. Search snippets are not pixel sources.
