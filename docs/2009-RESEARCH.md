## One-line thesis

**2009 is when the social web goes mainstream and apps become daily habit:** **Twitter** leaves the geek corner; **Facebook** ships **Like**, buys **FriendFeed**, passes **MySpace**, and kills **Beacon**; **FarmVille** invents mass social-game spam; **iPhone 3GS** + **OS 3.0** + **50k+ apps / 1B+ downloads** make the App Store the default smartphone story; **Android** multiplies handsets; **Bing** rebrands Microsoft search; **Windows 7** rescues the PC; **Kickstarter**, **Foursquare**, **WhatsApp**, and **UberCab** seed the 2010s — while most people still live on a **laptop**.
