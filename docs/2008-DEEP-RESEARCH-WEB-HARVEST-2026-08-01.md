## 0. One-line thesis (locked)

**2008 is the year of app stores and browser reinvention:** Apple ships **iPhone 3G** + **App Store** (native apps); **Android** reaches pockets (**T-Mobile G1 / HTC Dream**); **Chrome** launches as a browser built for web apps; **Firefox 3** peaks indie desktop loyalty; streaming (**Hulu** public · Netflix densify · **Spotify Europe**) and social login (**Facebook Connect** · **Google Friend Connect**) reshape how people arrive at services. Still mostly a **PC web** year — gatekeepers *begin*, they do not finish.

## 1. Scale & macro feel (primary numbers)

### 1.1 Internet Live Stats (June class)

Source: [Total number of Websites](https://www.internetlivestats.com/total-number-of-websites/) (NetCraft + Live Stats elaboration).

| Metric | 2007 | **2008** | Delta |
|--------|-----:|---------:|------:|
| Websites (June) | 121,892,559 | **172,338,726** | **+41%** |
| Internet users | 1,373,327,790 | **1,571,601,630** | ~+14% |
| Users per website | 11.3 | **9.1** | denser web |
| Chart “Websites launched” birthmark | Tumblr | **[Dropbox](https://web.archive.org/web/20080324155155/http://www.getdropbox.com/)** | Live Stats column |

**Museum labels (do not invent):**

- Sites: **172,338,726**  
- Users: **~1.57B**  
- Birthmark product: **Dropbox** (found 2007, public story / Live Stats 2008 mark)

### 1.2 Daily ritual (exhibit voice)

Boot **Windows XP** (still mass) · open **IE 7** (or Firefox if geek) · Digg / Facebook / YouTube · open Gmail (open since 2007) · from **March** maybe **Hulu** after dinner · from **July** friends with **iPhone 3G** talk about **apps** · **September** Chrome comic week · try Windows beta · late year if T-Mobile maybe **G1** · Spotify only if Europe + invite · most of the world still fat laptop.

### 1.3 Macro backdrop (not the product spine, but mood)

- Global financial crisis peaks late 2008 — online ad markets stressed; Google relatively resilient vs display-heavy Yahoo.  
- Web still grows hard (+41% sites) despite crisis.  
- Mobile *platform* story begins; **traffic** still overwhelmingly desktop.
