# 1. What “quality + working” means here

A fact is **quality** if it is dated, labeled, visited at a primary, and can become copy or a gate without inventing a number.

A kit is **working** if:

1. Selectors exist on the live HTML.
2. JS actually binds those selectors.
3. Incomplete never writes `{ multiStep, real, year, ts }`.
4. Next appears only after REAL.
5. A Playwright recipe can click the same path.

A room that has a Save button and a test, but **no boot**, is not research depth. It is a broken machine.
