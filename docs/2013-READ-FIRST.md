## 1. One-line thesis (locked draft)

**2013 is when the mobile internet becomes the default cultural layer — and privacy becomes a mass story:** **Vine** (6-second loops, Jan) and **Instagram Video** (15s, Jun) invent short-form video culture; **Snapchat Stories** (Oct) invents the 24-hour ephemeral feed; **iOS 7** (Sep) kills skeuomorphism for flat design; **iPhone 5s** ships **Touch ID** + **A7 64-bit** beside colorful **5c**; **Windows 8.1** tries to fix Win8’s Start problem; **PS4 / Xbox One** open the “next-gen” living room; **Edward Snowden** / **PRISM** force the open web to confront surveillance; **Bitcoin** has its first mainstream boom-bust-and-raid year (Silk Road) — while **Instagram Stories / Reels / TikTok / Reactions / Meta branding do not exist**, and **desktop Chrome** is already #1 even as apps keep eating the browser.

### Mood (period voice)

> Six-second loops everywhere · “did you see that Vine?” · gold iPhone and plastic 5c · flat icons overnight · fingerprint unlock · Snapchat Story at the end of the day · NSA slides on every homepage · Bitcoin on the news · Facebook Home was a flop · PS4 vs Xbox One arguments · Healthcare.gov won’t load.
