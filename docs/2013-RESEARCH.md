## One-line thesis

**2013 is the year short-form video, flat design, and mass surveillance awareness redefine the consumer internet:** Vine (6s) and Instagram Video (15s) create mobile video memes; Snapchat Stories invents the 24-hour ephemeral feed; iOS 7 flattens the phone UI; iPhone 5s adds Touch ID and 64-bit A7; Windows 8.1 patches the Start-button crisis; PS4 and Xbox One relaunch living-room platforms; Snowden/PRISM make privacy a front-page web story; Bitcoin has its first noisy boom year — while Reels, IG Stories (Meta), TikTok, and Reactions do not exist.
