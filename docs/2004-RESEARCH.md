## Thesis
**2004 is the hinge into Web 2.0:** **Gmail** (Apr) and **Flickr** reframe web apps; **Thefacebook** (Feb, Harvard) seeds campus social graphs; **Firefox 1.0** (Nov 9) challenges IE6; **Google IPO** makes search a public company story. Blogging + RSS are social software. Broadband is common; dial-up is not gone.
