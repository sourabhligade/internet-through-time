## 0. One-line thesis (locked)

**2012 is the year the internet becomes visual and app-first — and Web 2.0’s cultural era ends:** Facebook buys Instagram for **~$1B** days after Android launch; Facebook **IPOs** then hits **1 billion** users; Pinterest goes mass; YouTube professionalizes creators and **Gangnam Style** hits **1B views**; **iPhone 5** / **Lightning** / **iOS 6 Maps** and **iPad mini** land; **Windows 8** reimagines the desktop; **Chrome** takes the global browser crown from IE; **UberX** and **Snapchat** accelerate the next decade — while desktop still exists, **Stories/Reels/TikTok do not**, and the open web is increasingly subordinate to apps.
