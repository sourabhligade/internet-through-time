| **R** Reach | 1–3 | 3 = global mass · 2 = US/West or India-mass · 1 = specialist | Downloads, MAU, livestream, Pew, official press |

# 2. What 2016 actually felt like

You opened Instagram and there was a **new row of rings**. You still posted the pretty grid, but the goofy stuff went in Stories and **died in 24 hours**. Systrom said Snapchat deserved the credit. In July the **sidewalk was the game**: parks full of phones, batteries dead, servers melting. Facebook taught your thumb to **hold Like**. Apple **removed the headphone jack** and handed you a $9 dongle; AirPods were a December promise at $159. Vine said **nothing is happening today** and everyone knew it was over. WhatsApp grew a **green lock** for a billion people. For a Friday in October, **Twitter and Netflix would not load**. In India, Jio made 4G feel free until New Year’s Eve. The homepage also said **Einstein was right** (LIGO chirp), **a computer beat Lee Sedol on livestream**, **Juno reached Jupiter on July 4**, and **the nearest star has a planet**. Harambe and *Stranger Things* ate the feed. Leicester won the league at 5000–1. The Cavs came back from 3–1. The Cubs won. After November, Pew found **64%** of US adults thought fabricated news caused a great deal of confusion. TikTok’s logo was **not** on a Western home screen.

A 5-minute walk is a **success** if they say any three of: Stories showed up · everyone was outside with Pokémon GO · they took the headphone jack · Vine died · WhatsApp got the lock · the internet went down that Friday.

A 5-minute walk is a **fail** if they only remember “Microsoft bought LinkedIn” or “Google shipped Allo.”
